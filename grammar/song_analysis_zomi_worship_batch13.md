## Summary

**Songs 241–260** span 20 worship songs with themes of:
- **Faith & Trust**: Believing in God alone (246, 244, 252)
- **Praise & Worship**: Praising God's greatness (247, 250, 260)
- **God's Goodness**: His mercy and love (242, 248, 249)
- **Cross & Redemption**: The sacrifice of Christ (257, 255)
- **Faithfulness**: Being steadfast in God (253, 254)
- **God's Presence**: Resting in His bosom (251, 243)
- **Love & Covering**: Incomparable love (258, 259)
- **Joy & Celebration**: Happy day, eternal joy (241, 245)

**Key Grammar Patterns:**
- Future tense: `ding hi` (will be)
- Negation: `kei lo` (not), `ngei lo` (never)
- Ergative: `in` marking agents
- SOV word order throughout
- Contracted forms: `kong` = `ka` + `ong` (I will)

**Total new vocabulary**: ~120 words across 20 songs.
