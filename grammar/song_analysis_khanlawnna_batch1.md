## Song 1: JEHOVAH SHALOM NOPNA PASIAN

**Source:** Khanlawnna Late/1 - JEHOVAH SHALOM NOPNA PASIAN.txt  
**English:** Jehovah Shalom — God of Peace  
**Structure:** Verse → Chorus → V1 → Chorus → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text

**Verse:**
Jehovah Shalom nopna Pasian, ka up kasaan a kipan,
telzawh loh lungnopna nei ta'ng:
Nisim hihleitung nunna sungah, Nopna dahna kathuak zong,
Ka nopna Jehovah Shalom.

**Chorus:**
Aw! nuamsing Topa tungah, Aw! nuamsing Topa tungah,
Lungdamna lathak Topa kong sak ding;
Jesuh tawh ka kimuta, ahoihna katel siangta,
Kei ading kabangkim Jehovah Pasian.

**Verse 2:**
Ka mawhna khempeuh hong maisak Pa, Natna teng hong damsak Pa,
Ka muzo Jehovah Rapha;
Hih leitung neekzon haksatna leh, nate hang lunghiang nawnkeng,
Ka zawhna Jehovah Nissi.

**Verse 3:**
Jehovah Jire hongsikh Pasian, Tuciangciang hong huh Topa,
Kei ading amah hoih pen hi;
A maʼangliatna sung om hauhna, ka sapna teng hong cingsak,
Hong sikhPa, Jehovah Jire.

### Grammar Patterns
- **[GRAMMAR] Compound verb + directional:** `hong maisak` (DIR + forgive), `hong damsak` (DIR + heal), `hong cingsak` (DIR + lift) — hong indicates God as agent
- **[GRAMMAR] SOV:** `ka up kasaan a kipan` — Subject(ka up) + Predicate(kipan)
- **[GRAMMAR] Future marker:** `kong sak ding` — ding marks future intention
- **[GRAMMAR] Negation:** `Kei ading...kabangkim` — kei for negation of future possibility

### Vocabulary

| nuamsing | joy | nuam+sing (joy+tree) |
| lathak | new song | [COMPOUND] la+thak (song+new) |
| mawhna | sin | mawh+na |
| maisak | forgive | mai+sak |
| damsak | heal | dam+sak |
| nawnkeng | strong/firm | nawn+keng |
| hauhna | sufficiency | hauh+na |
| cingsak | lift up | cing+sak |
| lungdamna | joy | [COMPOUND] lung+dam+na |

### Poetic Devices
- **[POETIC] Hebrew names:** Jehovah Shalom (Peace), Rapha (Healer), Nissi (Victory), Jire (Provider) — each paired with Zolai translation
- **[POETIC] Anaphora:** "Topa" repeated at line endings across chorus
- **[POETIC] Epithet stacking:** `Nopna dahna kathuak zong` — abstract nouns as objects

### Themes
- Peace and healing; God as shalom, healer, provider
- Perseverance: standing firm in suffering
- Personal testimony: "ka muzo" (I have seen)

### Cultural Notes
- Hebrew divine names adapted into Zolai Christian hymn tradition
- "Pa" (Father) used directly to address God — intimate register

## Song 2: VAN LEH LEI PIANGSAK PASIAN

**Source:** Khanlawnna Late/2 - VAN LEH LEI PIANGSAK PASIAN.txt  
**English:** God Who Created Heaven and Earth  
**Structure:** Verse → Chorus → V1 → Chorus → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text

**Verse:**
Van leh lei piangsak Pasian, Ni leh kha piangsak Pasian,
Ka nuntakna a hongbawl ka Pasian;
Ni taang leh guahtui hongpia, tuk ciangin an hongla sak,
Na vangliatna ka phat hi ka Pasian.

**Chorus:**
Topa aw, ka lungdamhi, nisim nong lungdamsak hi,
Ka hinlai teng namin kaphatden ding;
Tumging tawh Nangkongphat ding,
laamnatawh Nangkongphatding,
Halelujah namin thang den ta hen.

**Verse 2:**
Tu ni-in, kei adingin hunhoih hunnuam hong kipia,
Topa tungӨ lungdamna ka ko ding hi;

Ka hatna teng tawh namin ka phat ding.

**Verse 3:**
Khat veivei neekding neilo, khat veivei silhding neilo,
A hihhang TopaҠhoihna kaphawk hi;
Van-a leeng vasa teleh, gamlak Unok paak te-in,
Ka nunna nisimin tha hong dimzeel.

## Song 3: HUNHOIH HUNNUAM

**Source:** Khanlawnna Late/3 - HUNHOIH HUNNUAM.txt  
**English:** Good Joy  
**Structure:** Verse → Chorus → V1 → Chorus → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text

**Verse:**
Hunhoih hunnuam hongmu sak, hun kilawm hongngahsak,
Hun manpha sung, hongtonpih ka To;
Zumleh hiam panin hongkem; lauhuai napan hongkem,
Cidamtakin aminpha kalo.

**Chorus:**
Lungdamsing hih hunpha sungah, ka Topa'n lamhongpii,
Nasi pha tawh hong naisakhi;
Na omnapeuh, lungnopna kanunna'n vaangnei,
Nongitna kei sungah, meibangin lumden hen,
Halelujah aminthang hen.

**Verse 2:**
Silh leh teen, anneek tuidawn, nisim hongsikh hongvaak,
Abeitheilo hehpihna thupha;
Ka Kha adingzong honglui, Thu leh la tawh hongvaak,
Hici bangin hongluang a thupha.

**Verse 3:**
Lono hing silsial bangin, Unokpak palhbangin,
Kanuntakna hngnosuah sakzeel;
Pahtawi dingin kilawmpen, Phatdingin zong hoihpen,

**English:** I Will Give Joy to the Lord
