## 181. Nahum 1:7 — "Nahum 1:7"

### Lyrics
```
Verse 1:
Ka nunna hu hong muam Pa / Kei hong bawl ka Pasian
Ka lungsim tawng dong nong tel hi / Kei nong lawn, nong hopih na ten'n
Ka thahat na hi / Ka kicin loh Nong thei hi
Ka thanem Nong tel hi / Hih hang'n Nang kong ki sam pha hi

Chorus:
Topa hoih hi, lungkham hun in / A mite huh tawntung hi
Amah a bel mite limtak / Hum in kem hi
Vangik pua in a gim mite / Ama kiang ah tawldam na pia
Nang lo kisap dang om kei / A hoih Pasian

Verse 2:
Ka hun khempeuh a nei Pa / Ka husan na nei Pa
Ka thuak na khempeuh Nong thei hi / Ka tuk ciang Na liangko tung ah
Kei Nong pua to hi / Kei tung ah Nong thuman hi
Kei tung Nong citak hi / Nang ma theih loh a piang om kei
```

### Key Lines
| Zolai | Gloss |
|-------|-------|
| Ka nunna hu hong muam Pa | you protect my life's breath |
| Ka lungsim tawng dong nong tel hi | you hear the voice of my heart |
| Topa hoih hi, lungkham hun in | the Lord is good, in times of trouble |
| Hum in kem hi | he shelters with his wings |

### New Vocabulary
- **nunna** — life, soul
- **nunna hu** — breath of life
- **lungkham** — trouble, distress
- **huh** — to protect, shelter
- **hum** — wing
- **tawldam** — to rest, find peace
- **thuman** — to answer
- **citak** — to trust, rely on

### Themes
Divine protection and refuge in times of trouble. God shelters and comforts those who trust him.
