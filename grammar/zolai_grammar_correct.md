# Zolai Grammar — Correct Usage Based on Bible

> Extracted from Bible corpus (TDB77/ZVS) for learning purposes.
> Last updated: 2026-09-07

## 1. Verb Patterns

### A. "ka ne" (I eat) — WITHOUT "an"

**Correct patterns from Bible:**
- `ka ne ngei het kei ding hi` = I will not be able to eat (1CO 9:13)
- `ka ne ka dawn kei ding hi` = I will not eat (1KI 13:8)
- `ka ne ngei kei hi` = I have never eaten (ACT 10)
- `ka ne kei a` = I do not eat (1SA 1:15)

**Key insight:** "ka ne" is the correct form for "I eat". Do NOT use "ka an ne".

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| I will not eat | `ka ne ka dawn kei ding hi` | 1KI 13:8 |
| I have never eaten | `ka ne ngei kei hi` | ACT 10 |
| I do not eat | `ka ne kei a` | 1SA 1:15 |
| I will not be able to eat | `ka ne ngei het kei ding hi` | 1CO 9:13 |

### B. "nek" Usage — Only in Specific Constructions

**Correct "nek" patterns:**
- `a nek khit phetun` = immediately after eating (GEN)
- `an ka nek leh` = when I eat food (1CO 10)

**Key insight:** "nek" is used in specific constructions, not as a general verb marker.

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Immediately after eating | `a nek khit phetun` | GEN |
| When I eat food | `an ka nek leh` | 1CO 10 |

### C. "ka pai kei" (I don't go) — Correct Negation

**Correct patterns from Bible:**
- `ka pai kei a` = I do not go (GAL 1:16)
- `ka pai kei ding` = I will not go (GEN 49:6)
- `ka kizom kei ding hi` = I will not associate (GEN 49:6)

**Key insight:** Use "kei" for 1st/2nd person negation, NOT "lo".

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| I do not go | `ka pai kei a` | GAL 1:16 |
| I will not go | `ka pai kei ding` | GEN 49:6 |
| I will not associate | `ka kizom kei ding hi` | GEN 49:6 |

### D. "lo ding" (will not) — Future Negation

**Correct patterns from Bible:**
- `pua thei lo ding hi` = will not be able to read (1CH 15:2)
- `kilok ngeilo ding hi` = will not be ashamed (1CH 16:30)
- `beisak nawnlo ding uh hi` = will not be despised (1CH 17:10)
- `hong tamasaklo ding hi` = will not torment (1CH 28:20)

**Key insight:** "lo ding" is used for future negation with ability/possibility.

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Will not be able to read | `pua thei lo ding hi` | 1CH 15:2 |
| Will not be ashamed | `kilok ngeilo ding hi` | 1CH 16:30 |
| Will not be despised | `beisak nawnlo ding uh hi` | 1CH 17:10 |
| Will not torment | `hong tamasaklo ding hi` | 1CH 28:20 |

## 2. Question Patterns

### A. "bang hang" (why) — Content Question

**Correct patterns from Bible:**
- `bang hang hiam cih leh` = why (1CH 1:19, 1CH 4:40, 1CH 5:20)
- `bang hangin` = because (1CH 5:20)

**Key insight:** "bang hang" is used for "why" questions.

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Why | `bang hang hiam cih leh` | 1CH 1:19 |
| Because | `bang hangin` | 1CH 5:20 |

### B. "diam" — Future/Soft Question

**Correct patterns from Bible:**
- `diamdiam ding-te` = will surely (2CO 8)

**Key insight:** "diam" is used for soft/future questions.

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Will surely | `diamdiam ding-te` | 2CO 8 |

### C. "hiam" — Yes/No Question

**Correct patterns from Bible:**
- `ka hi hiam?` = am I? (1CH 11:19)
- `ka hi hiam?` = am I? (1CH 13:12)
- `hong pai ding hiam?` = will he come? (1CH 14:10)

**Key insight:** "hiam" is the universal question marker for yes/no questions.

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Source |
|---------|-------|--------|
| Am I? | `ka hi hiam?` | 1CH 11:19 |
| Will he come? | `hong pai ding hiam?` | 1CH 14:10 |

## 3. Sentence Structures

### A. SOV (Subject-Object-Verb)

**Basic pattern:** S + O + V

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Structure |
|---------|-------|-----------|
| God created the world | `Pasian leitung bawl hi` | S + O + V |
| I eat rice | `Ka ne hi` | S + V |
| He reads the book | `Amah laibu sim hi` | S + O + V |

### B. Ergative Marker "in"

**Usage:** Marks the agent/subject of transitive verbs

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Note |
|---------|-------|------|
| He reads the book | `Amah in laibu sim hi` | Emphatic subject |
| The man eats rice | `Mipa in ne hi` | Agent marked |
| God created the world | `Pasian in leitung bawl hi` | Agent marked |

### C. Negation Patterns

**1st/2nd person:** Use "kei"
**3rd person:** Use "lo" or "kei"

**Examples:**
| English | Zolai | Person |
|---------|-------|--------|
| I don't go | `Ka pai kei hi` | 1st |
| You don't go | `Na pai kei hi` | 2nd |
| He doesn't go | `A pai lo hi` | 3rd |
| He doesn't go | `A pai kei hi` | 3rd (alternative) |

## 4. Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Incorrect: "ka an ne hi"
**Correct:** `Ka ne hi` (I eat)

### ❌ Incorrect: "Ka pai lo hi" (for 1st person)
**Correct:** `Ka pai kei hi` (I don't go)

### ❌ Incorrect: "Ka pai nek hi" (I go to eat)
**Correct:** `Ka pai hi` (I go) or `Ka nek ding hi` (I will eat)

### ❌ Incorrect: "na pai hiam?" (for future)
**Correct:** `Na pai diam?` (Will you go?)

## 5. Learning Examples

### Basic Sentences
| English | Zolai | Pattern |
|---------|-------|---------|
| I eat | `Ka ne hi` | S + V |
| I go | `Ka pai hi` | S + V |
| I read | `Ka sim hi` | S + V |
| I don't eat | `Ka ne kei hi` | S + V + neg |
| I don't go | `Ka pai kei hi` | S + V + neg |
| I will eat | `Ka ne ding hi` | S + V + future |
| I will go | `Ka pai ding hi` | S + V + future |
| I will not eat | `Ka ne kei ding hi` | S + V + future neg |
| I will not go | `Ka pai kei ding hi` | S + V + future neg |


### ⚠️ Important Note on Future Negation
**Future negation ALWAYS uses `kei + ding` (regardless of person):**
- ✅ `Ka pai kei ding hi.` — I will not go. (1st person)
- ✅ `A pai kei ding hi.` — He will not go. (3rd person)
- ❌ `Ka pai lo ding hi.` — Unnatural
- ❌ `A pai lo ding hi.` — Unnatural

### Questions

|---------|-------|---------|

| Do you go? | `Na pai hiam?` | V + question |
| Will you eat? | `Na ne diam?` | V + future question |
| Will you go? | `Na pai diam?` | V + future question |
| Why do you go? | `Bang hang na pai hiam?` | why + V + question |
