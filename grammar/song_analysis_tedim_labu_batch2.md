## Song 21: HONG KHIHNA IN A PHA HI (TDM - 21)

**English:** Blest be the Tie that Binds
**Reference:** Matt. 11:30
**Structure:** V1 → V2 → V3 → V4 → V5

### Full Text

**Verse 1:**
Ei lung hong khih nading, Jesuh itna pha hi;
Lung kituak a kikholhna in, Vantung itna lim hi.

**Verse 2:**
Ei Pa tokhom mai ah, Nakpi'n thu i ngen hi;
Ei lawpna, upna deihna teng, A kibang in om hi.

**Verse 3:**
Ei te'n dahna thuak khawm, Van puak na ki pua hi;
Khat veivei i lawmte a' ding, Khitui a luang thei hi.

**Verse 4:**
Ei te ki khen ciang in, Lungsim dahna piang hi;
Hih hang i lungsim tawh om khawm, Nungciang ki mu ding hi.

**Verse 5:**
Hih bang lametna in, Lungsim hong kip sak hi;
Pasian um a i muan na in, Lungnop ki cin na hi.

### Word-by-Word (Key Lines)

| Line | Zolai | Morpheme Break | English |
|------|-------|----------------|---------|
| V1 | Ei lung hong khih nading | ei (our) + lung (heart) + hong (DIR) + khih (bind) + na+ding (NMZ+FUT) | To bind our hearts together |
| V1 | Jesuh itna pha hi | Jesuh + it+na (love+NMZ) + pha (ripe/strong) + hi | Jesus's love is strong |
| V2 | Ei Pa tokhom mai ah | ei (our) + Pa (Father) + tok+homb (before+time) + mai (face/before) + ah (LOC) | In the presence of our Father |
| V3 | Khat veivei i lawmte a' ding | khat (one) + vei+vei (each+every) + i (our) + lawm+te (joy+PL) + a+ding (will+be) | Each of our joys will be shared |
| V5 | Pasian um a i muan na in | Pasian + um (exist) + a (3SG.AGR) + i (our) + muan+na (hope+NMZ) + in (ERG) | Since God exists, our hope remains |

### New Vocabulary

| Word | English | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| khih | bind/tie | binding connection |
| lametna | comfort/consolation | lam+et+na |
| nopna | peace | no+pi+na |
| kikholhna | fellowship | ki+kholh+na (RECIP+join+NMZ) |
| kituak | united | ki+tuak (RECIP+join) |

### Themes
- Christian fellowship and spiritual unity
- Shared suffering and joy among believers
- God's presence as source of comfort and hope
- Eternal reunion after earthly separation
