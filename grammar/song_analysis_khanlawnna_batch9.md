## Song 161 — NANG BANG OM LO HI
**Title:** Nang Bang Om Lo Hi
**English:** How Can You Not Be

### Lyrics
```
Nang bang om lo hi, Nang bang a ka lungsim sukha om kei;
A tawntung tawntung in ka zonzon hang, Mu keng Nang bang om lo hi.

Nong hehpihna Guntui bang in luang, Na liamma hang nat na dam zo,
Haksat na pan Nisim nong liah nong dal Nang bang om lo hi.
```

### Word-by-Word (Key Lines)
| Zolai | Gloss |
|-------|-------|
| Nang | you |
| bang | how |
| om lo hi | cannot be |
| lungsim | heart/spirit |
| sukha | happy/peaceful |
| zonzon hang | always seeking |
| hehpihna | rejoicing |
| Guntui | Gethsemane |
| haksat | suffering |
| Nisim | nails |

### New Vocabulary
- `sukha` — happy, peaceful (loan from Hindi/Sanskrit)
- `Guntui` — Gethsemane (biblical place name)
- `Nisim` — nails (biblical imagery)

### Themes
- Jesus' suffering at Gethsemane, contrast between joy and pain, wonder at God's love

```
Tuan a simthu sut leng maw
Tual i niam simthu
Theihphaklouh selung mawlte ihi,
Tung Pathian itna vangin
Ei din' khua ahongvak
Tun a tate hi hang e.

Phatna zaila lawm ging
Akilawm semgua toh
Vanglian Toupa min phat in
Tuan a selung mawlte tun a hon domsang ta
Toupa ei din hoih na e.
```

### New Vocabulary
- `simthu` — story, tale
- `theihphaklouh` — unfathomable, beyond understanding
- `selung` — mercy, grace
- `ahongvak` — has arrived (honorific)
- `domsang` — welcomed, received

### Themes
- God's unfathomable mercy, welcoming home, praise for God's faithfulness
