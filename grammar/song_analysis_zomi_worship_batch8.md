## 141. Lau Khasiangtho Aw — "Praise the King of Kings"

### Lyrics
```
Lau Khasiangtho aw mun hong luah aw
Mikim tung ah hong ki buah in aw
A keugaw nuntakna hong lawng aw
Guah bang in hih mun ah hong zu aw

Chorus: Khasiangtho aw / Nang lo in bang mah hi keng e / Khasiangtho aw / Nang lo in bang mah hih thei keng
Bridge: Guahbang hong zu suk aw / Meibang in hong kuang aw / Huihpi bang in hong nung in maw
```

### Key Lines Word-by-Word
| Zolai | POS | Gloss |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Lau Khasiangtho aw | V+V+PTCL | praise King-of-Kings PTCL |
| Mikim tung ah | N+POST | on my knees |
| A keugaw nuntakna | N+POSS+N | his precious life |
| Guah bang in | N+Q+PTCL | like what (how) |

### New Vocabulary
- **khasiangtho** — King of Kings (kha king + siang holy + tho suffix)
- **keugaw** — precious
- **guah bang** — how, in what way
- **meibang** — with fire
- **huihpi** — with tears

### Themes
Praise, desperation, surrender — calling on God's presence with tears and fire.
