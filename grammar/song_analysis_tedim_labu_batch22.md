**Common Vocabulary across batch:** `vantung` (heaven), `Topa` (Lord), `Pasian` (God), `Jesuh` (Jesus), `lungdam` (joy), `dahna` (sorrow), `sihna` (death), `inn` (house/home), `gam` (land), `nopna` (joy/happiness)

**Grammar Patterns Observed:**
- Heavy use of `ding` (FUT) particle — future hope emphasis
- `nawn lo` / `nawnlohna` — "no more" negation pattern
- `tawh` (with) — accompaniment for heavenly reunion
- `in` (ERG) — divine agency (Jesuh in, Topa in, Pasian in)
- `ciangin` (after/when) — temporal subordination for eschatological events
