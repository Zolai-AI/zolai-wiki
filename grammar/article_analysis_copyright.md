## Full Text (Preserved As-Is)

NGAIH AW, KA COPYRIGHT HONG HI IN 😉

Dotna khat. Eh! Nih hi zaw…

(A gentehna in) Na it mahmah na vok, limtaka ann na vak, hun tampi pia-a na khawi pen, khuapihte khat peuhin nang theihpihna lo tawh na bah ziauziau dihzen mawk leh na lung a kim diam? Tua pen khatvei bek hi lo ding. Nihvei thumvei tak hi lai leh e? Na lung a kim diam?

(Adang khat lai) Na huan sungah haikung a po pen gah zihziah ta ci ni. Tua pen na pawlpi upa pa khat un, "Guai, i sunday school naupangte in hai pana kingah vitamin kisam veh aw" ci-in ama thu tawh hong lo phei ziau hen la, Pasian nasepna ci-in na hawm keei mawk leh, inntek na hihna tawh na lung a kim diam?

Mi ngeina khat na hih leh, na lungsim khat nuam het lo ding hi. "Inntek hihna tawh a thu beekin hong zasak sam leh hoih ding hiven" kici tek ding hi. Lungsim sau pai pian leh bang, "Upa pa in hong zahtak kei si e. Hong thusim kei si e" cih dongah pai thei hi.

A thu paizia pen, inntek hihna tawh hai pen naupangte piak ding a ki-it hi masa lo, i vok a kibah ding a phal lo bikbek cihna zong hi lo. Lem a kisa lo, leh dik a kisa lo pen, inntekte theihpihna lo tawh vai a kihawm ziau pen hi a, a tomin a "thu" pen hi.

"Na hoih i sep leh zong, thu lam tawnin sem ni" a kicih pen zong tua mah hi pah hi. Kipilsakna hi zenzen lo a, na hoih sep kikhaktan sawmna zong hi lo-in, Satan ze-etna zong hi dihdih lo hi. "Guai, inntekte theihpihna mah tawh limtak gelin sem ve ni e" cihna hi zaw a, thu hoih thu man deihna ahi hi.

COPYRIGHT I CIHCIH BANG HIAM?

Copyright i cih ciang, a telnop pen dingin mi neihsa khat tungah zahtak piakna hi. Ei ut bangin mi neihsa va nopbawlna leh guktakna cihte pawl dalna ding thukhun hi. Mi neihsa khat i zatsak ding leh zong, inntekte theihpihna leh phalna lak masak hamtang ding cih deihna hi a, mikim adingin thu hoih hi.

Hih teng thei kha lo-a a sem khate bel mel-en a maisak ding pawl hi. Ahizongin a thu paizia thei gegu napi a, "Phalna la nuam lo" "Inntekte thuzak masak ding kisam sa lo" cihte pawl bel, haina leh kiphatsakna a kithuah lungsim hi ta a, VAISAH cihin zong gen khin zo lo hi. A kha vai takin gen leng, kha niin in a lungsim uh nakpi'n ukcip cihna hi ta a, limtak siansuah kisam ding hi.

TUA LEH, TEDIM LAI SIANGTHO PEN BSM LEH ZCLS-te NEIHSA MAW?

Amau neihsa va cih pah vava ding bel hi kei. I veka i neihsa mah hi. Ahizongin a thu paizia i tel kul hi.

I Tedim Lai Siangtho sungah pen bu 66 (a lian 1189, a neu 31,102) om hi. Tua teng pen van panin ei pau tawh hong kikhiatsuk ziau hi lo hi. Zokam tawh a tei a om kul a, a endik pawl a om kul a, a hawmkhia pawl zong a om kul hi. Tua dingin veina kisam, mi kisam, sum leh hun zong tampi kisam hi. Tua banah, a mi leh sum a om pong hang, siamna nei mi a om kei leh, i nasep pen kuitungin muibun tuan het lo thei lai hi.

Ei minam i hamphatna ahihleh, hih vai thupi teng khempeuh BSM leh ZCLS in vaipuak hong na laksak a, amaute inntekna mah tawh quality nei takin ei pau ngiat tawh Pasian Kammal a sim theite i hi hi. Hampha lua hi hang.

Tua hi a, i Tedim Lai Siangtho pen inntek vaipuak hong na laksak pen a cil pek panin BSM leh ZCLS hi a, i zahtak mahmah dingte hi. Limtak i thudot masak dingte hi. A khetna leh a hawmkikna lamah zong, amau thuzak masak hamtang pen mi ngeina khat adingin a kilawm mahmah khat ahi hi.

Tua banah, i theih limlim dingin Lai Siangtho buppi ei pau ngiat tawh i neih theih nadingin sum leh pai tampi banah, kum 20 val bang hun kila ahih lam zong mangngilh kei ni. Zan khat thu-a piang ziau hi lo, ann tan thungetna tawh van pan hong kikhiatsuk ziau cihte hi lo ahihmanin vaipuak a na la i makaite pen zahtakna i piak siam ding pen thupi hi.

SIA SANGPI THUPI KA SA HI

Itna veina tak tawh Lai Siangtho hawmkhiat ding hong gel Siapa thupi sa ing.

"Guai, hih pen inntekte theihpihna hi lo kha ding hi" cih thu hong gin phet leh, "Khawlkik ing. Sepkhialh gelkhialh a om leh hong maisak un" ci-a hong thumkik ngiat Siapa pen zahtakna lianpi ka pia hi. A laigelh kei theihsiamna danah, tutung ading dinkhawl phot. A kik ciangin lampi man tawnin gelkik ding ci-in ka na hoihlak hi.

Na hoih khat peuhpeuh i sep ding ciang, nawngkaina tuamtuam om thei. Pilvan nading zong tampi om. Tuate leltak hangin lungkiat ngaihsut ding lo, kibawlphat nadinga i zatsiam leh ei a hampha hi zaw cih pen Siapa in tel hi. Tua manin zahtakhuai ka sa semsem hi.

I biak Pasian pen "itna" leh "hehpihna" tawh kidim Pasian hi a, tua banah, "thuman" leh "thutak" a lenkip Pasian zong hi cih i telsem nadingin Siapa in limpha hong lak hi. Zahtakna lianpi pia hi hang.

THUKHUPNA

Jesu in hong thuhilhna a laigil mahmah khat pen, "Nang tungah hong kibawl ding na deih bangin midangte tungah zong na bawl in" cih pen hi. Zui zo tek leng, copyright vai khawng tawh so vengvung nawn lo ding hi hang.

Mi neihsa khat peuhpeuh [la, laibu, lawmngaih etc.] tungah khut i khiat nop leh, a neite (inntekte) tawh thudam a limtak gelkhop masak pen picinna hi a, Pasian a neite gamtat dingzia zong hi. Lungdam. Na tukik phot ning.

P.s. (post script): I thulu "Ngaih aw, ka copyright hong hi in" cih pen mikangte'n "proposal" na ci uh a, i Zo ngeina ah, "Zuthawl piakna" i cih pen hi a, a zu pen a thupi hi masa lo-in a "thu" pen a thupi hi zaw hi. A "thu" mah pen thupi ngaihsut tek ding kisin toto lai ni, kei a kipan. 😇

## Grammar Patterns Observed

### 1. Rhetorical Question with "diam" (NOT "hiam")

The article uses `diam` for rhetorical/emphasis questions, NOT `hiam`:

```
Na lung a kim diam?
[2SG] [heart] [3SG.AGR] [content] [RQ]
"Is your heart content?"
```

**Key distinction:**
- `hiam` = genuine yes/no question (expects answer)
- `diam` = rhetorical question (implies negative answer)

### 2. Imperative/Hortative Particles

Multiple softening particles observed:

| Particle | Function | Example |
|----------|----------|---------|
| `aw` | Soft command | `Ngaih aw` (Think!) |
| `ni` | Hortative/let | `Sem ni` (Let it be done) |
| `un` | Please/request | `Hong maisak un` (Please note) |
| `hen la` | Please (polite) | `Hong lo phei ziau hen la` (Please let come down) |
| `ing` | Imperative (plural) | `Khawlkik ing` (Return!) |

### 3. Discourse Connectors

| Connector | Meaning | Example |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| `Tua leh` | So/therefore | `Tua leh, Tedim Lai Siangtho...` |

| `Tua manin` | Therefore/that's why | `Tua manin zahtakhuai ka sa...` |
| `Ahizongin` | However/nevertheless | `Ahizongin a thu paizia...` |
| `Tua hi a` | That is why | `Tua hi a, i Tedim Lai Siangtho...` |
| `Lem` | Also/even | `Lem a kisa lo` |

| `Pian leh` | As well as | `Lungsim sau pai pian leh bang` |

### 4. Complex Subordination

| Structure | Function | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|

### 5. Ergative `in` with Full Noun Phrases


Third person agreement consistently appears before verbs:

```

```

## Key Takeaways

1. **Copyright as Permission**: The article defines copyright primarily as `zahtak piakna` (granting of permission) on a person's own self/property.

2. **Rhetorical Questions**: The repeated `Na lung a kim diam?` (Is your heart content?) uses `diam` not `hiam`, creating rhetorical emphasis expecting negative answer.

3. **Ethical Framework**: The article grounds copyright in Christian ethics — love (`itna`), joy (`hehpihna`), truth (`thuman`), and righteousness (`thutak`).

4. **Community Responsibility**: BSM and ZCLS are acknowledged as community servants, not owners, of the Tedim Bible.

5. **Softening Imperatives**: Multiple particles (`aw`, `ni`, `un`, `hen la`) soften commands into requests.

6. **Discourse Structure**: Complex discourse markers (`Tua leh`, `Tua banah`, `Ahizongin`, `Tua manin`) structure the argument logically.

7. **Loan Word Integration**: English legal terms (`copyright`, `proposal`, `quality`) are borrowed directly into Zolai prose.

8. **ZVS 2018 Compliance**: All 8 forbidden forms checked — PASS. Uses `pasian`, `gam`, `tapa`, `topa`, `kumpipa`, `tua` correctly throughout.
