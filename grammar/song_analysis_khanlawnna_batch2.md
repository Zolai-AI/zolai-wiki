## Song 21: VAN LAPI

**Source:** Khanlawnna Late/21 - VAN LAPI.txt  
**English:** Heavenly City / City in the Sky  
**Structure:** Verse → Chorus → V1 → Chorus → V2 → Chorus → Coda

### Full Text

**Verse:**
Kei tumtakin lundamna lasa in kapai hi,
Khat veivei kalungzuang in kapphun kha zelzel hi;
Hibang dinmun ciangbek tawh, vaakthap lel dingmaw,
Vantungmite tawh vanlapi sa-khawm lo dingmaw.

**Chorus:**
Mualsang tungpan in, La-aw galzak ing, Amah
muangin mangleng, kei zong sathei ding hi'ing,
Tua lungdamna Thu gen-in, kavak kawmin ah,
Limtak in kangaih takciangin, van lapi kaza.

**Verse 2:**
Lungdamna Thu gimp-a, gen baih kei mahinteh,
Na kipat khiat dingzong, baihkei thei khamah inteh;
Hih hang kipan phot leteh, natel nading hi,
Van lapi a kisakna mun, Zion zuan na hi.

**Coda:**
Nitang sung om dihin, tawntung nitang sung omin,
Nitang sungah nungta dih in;
Tawntung nuamnasa dinghi, Nalam hong tangsak dinghi,
Sanggam aw, nitaang sungah omdihin.

| V1 | Vaakthap lel dingmaw | vaak+thap (mouth+together) + lel (carry) + ding+maw (FUT+Q) | Shall we carry it together on our lips? |
