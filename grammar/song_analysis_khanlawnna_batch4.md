## Song 61: LEITUNG MITTAWH MEL KAMUH LOH HANG

**English:** Beyond Earth, a Song Without End
**Structure:** Verse → V1 → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text
**V1:** Leitung mittawh mel kamuhloh hang, Vanglian Pa' khutin, paii-bang hongpom den; Naubang hong kepna te, hambang honghuai nate, Anoop innuam semsem Halelujah.
**V2:** Leitung pilna bangmah neilo a, Lungmawl kei banglel, mimcii bang nongteel, Sian aw, naleenggahhuan, giabang zuun kacimlo, Lau Khasiangtho tuibang hong dimden aw.
**Chorus:** Tung solkhapi amial zong, Tung sunni mual aliam zong, Mualliam theilo kei nongitna; Cinglo, taaklo, tualniam kanunna, A saangna lam hong kaito, AGAPE itna Pasian, na Min paakbang Kong tawina, zaila'n naubang hong awi awi ning aw.
**V3:** Patpuan hilo, naipuan hilo-a, Zeisu'sisan tawh, kizem puan silhta'ng; Lungin kageelgeel ciang, hoihin ahoih semsem, An bang hongitna lian tumdang diak sa'ng.
