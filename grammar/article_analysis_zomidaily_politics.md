## Full Text (Preserved As-Is)

GZA Youth Forum ah thusunna Paper

Thu Patna: Ihun ngah lah tom peuh mahin igen ding thulu lah zailua ahih manin, atomin sut ni cih ong gen masa nuam ing. Gamvai leh ki-ukna vai (Politics) cih ciangin leitung mihingte'khuasakzia khempeuh tawh akisai ban ah nisim nuntakzonna, neekzonna ah mihingte ikizopna khempeuh politics nuai ah aom ahihi. Leitungah mihing a omlai teng buaina (problem) om ding aa tua mihingte'buaina alemtuah ding leh kisapnate akicingsak dingin politics kisem ahihi. Na khempeuh kizom dimdiam tek(inter-related) aa, tua khempeuh abuptuah in politics ahi hi. Lamgei ah mehteh mehgah azuak nupinu leh anbuukah an ahuan nupinu zong gamthu hoih kei leh sukha veve hi.

Politics hoih kei leh pilna sinte in zong angah zah ding uh hamphatna vanzatte akipan pilna ngahzolo uh aa, nasemte in zong nasemta leh asepzah un thaman ngahlo uh hi. Papi khat in atate sang akahsak zawhlohna, an akhamhsak zawhlohna pen politics hoih leh sia tawh kisai mahmah hi. Zawnna leh tualniamnate zong politics ahoih loh man mah abulpi hizaw hi. Biakna, ngeina, pilsinna, nasepna, sumbawlna, nuntak khuasakna ah kideidanna leh apilte in apilna bangin thaman hamphatna angahna leh angahlohna pen gamvai leh gam ki-ukna hoih leh hoihlo tungah kingah hi gige hi.

Gamvai leh ki-ukna ah panlakna leh gualtung tuanna anei minamte in gamkhat a ut bangbang un zeek thei uh hi. Tua ahih manin, gamthu leh ki-ukna athudonlo minam leh gamte khangtolo in mite nuai ah ong den tawntung uh hi. Tua hi aa, ei khangthak Zomi momnote in politics ah munluah in ithudon ding kisam mahmah ta hi. Politics I uuk kei aa ikin kei leh mite khutsung kitung ding hi.

## Grammar Patterns

### 1. Definitional Construction with "cih ciangin"
- **Pattern**: Term + `cih ciangin` + Definition
- **Example**: `Gamvai leh ki-ukna vai cih ciangin...`
- **Note**: `cih ciangin` = "when we say" — used for defining/explaining terms

### 2. Abstract Agent with "pen"
- **Pattern**: Noun + `pen` + Predicate
- **Example**: `politics hoih leh sia tawh kisai mahmah hi` (politics is related to good and bad)
- **Note**: `pen` marks the topic/subject of a definitional statement

### 3. List Construction
- **Pattern**: Noun₁ + Noun₂ + ... + Noun_n + Verb
- **Example**: `Biakna, ngeina, pilsinna, nasepna, sumbawlna, nuntak khuasakna ah`
- **Note**: Lists of 3+ items use comma separation — common in political/academic prose

### 4. Conditional + Consequence
- **Pattern**: Verb + `kei aa` + Verb + `kei leh` + Consequence
- **Example**: `Politics I uuk kei aa ikin kei leh mite khutsung kitung ding hi`
- **Note**: Double negative condition = emphatic consequence

### 5. Causal Connector "ahih manin"
- **Pattern**: Cause + `ahih manin` + Result
- **Example**: `Tua ahih manin, gamthu leh ki-ukna...`
- **Note**: Most common discourse connector in modern Zolai (23,856 occurrences)
