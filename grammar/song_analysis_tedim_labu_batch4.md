## Song 61: HONPA' HONG MAKAIH BANG

**Structure:** V1 → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text

**V3:** Ka zui ding hi, Gumpa hong lah mahbangin, Dah taleng, nuam taleng, lungdam in zui ning; Gum pa'n thei hi, a muanhuai thuman lampi, A tang kham khuapi sungah hong tun pih ding.
