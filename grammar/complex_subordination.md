## 1. Quotative Structures

### "ci-in" — Quotative-Purposive

**Function:** Links reported speech/action to a following verb. Can be quotative ("saying") or purposive ("in order to").

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | "Guai, i sunday school naupangte in hai pana kingah vitamin kisam veh aw" **ci-in** ama thu tawh hong lo phei ziau hen la | "Hey, our Sunday school children need vitamins from there" **saying**, with his word, please let it come down | Quotative |
| 2 | Pasian nasepna **ci-in** na hawm keei mawk leh | **for** God's work, you print it fully | Purposive |
| 3 | Lampi man tawnin gelkik ding **ci-in** ka na hoihlak hi | saying it will move back in full manner, I desire it for you | Quotative |

**Pattern:** `"[Quote]" ci-in [action].`

**Analysis:**
- `ci` = quotative particle
- `in` = imperfective/progressive marker
- Together = "saying" or "in order to"

---

### "cih" — Quotative Complement

**Function:** Introduces a quoted complement clause.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | "Upa pa in hong zahtak kei si e. Hong thusim kei si e" **cih** dongah pai thei hi | "The elder has not permitted. Has not agreed" **QUOT** there-to go-can-DECL | Quotative complement |
| 2 | "Na hoih i sep leh zong, thu lam tawnin sem ni" **a kicih** pen zong tua mah hi pah hi | "If you do good also, do it in the manner of the word" **what is called** that is still the same | Nominalized quote |

**Pattern:** `"[Quote]" cih [verb].`

**Analysis:**
- `cih` = quotative particle (complement)
- Follows the quoted material
- Introduces the verb that reports the quote

---

### "ci-a" — Quotative (Variant)

**Function:** Variant of `ci-in` with slightly different aspect.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | "Khawlkik ing. Sepkhialh gelkhialh a om leh hong maisak un" **ci-a** hong thumkik ngiat | "Return. If there are errors, please note it" **saying**, emphasized indeed | Quotative |

**Pattern:** `"[Quote]" ci-a [action].`

## 2. Temporal/Conditional Clauses

### "ciang" — When / At the time of

**Function:** Marks temporal clauses — "when" something happens.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Na hoih khat peuhpeuh i sep **ding** ciang, nawngkaina tuamtuam om thei | When we each do good, various obstacles can exist | Temporal |
| 2 | A kik **ciangin** lampi man tawnin gelkik ding ci-in ka na hoihlak hi | When it returns, saying it will move back in full manner, I desire it for you | Temporal |

**Pattern:** `[Action] ciang(in), [subsequent event].`

**Analysis:**
- `ciang` = when (temporal)
- `ciangin` = when-IMPF (with imperfective)
- Introduces a temporal subordinate clause

---

### "leh" — If (Conditional)

**Function:** Marks conditional clauses — "if" something is true.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Na hoih i sep leh zong**, thu lam tawnin sem ni | **If you do good also**, do it in the manner of the word | Conditional |
| 2 | Sepkhialh gelkhialh **a om leh** hong maisak un | If there are errors, please note it | Conditional |
| 3 | Mi ngeina khat **na hih leh**, na lungsim khat nuam het lo ding hi | If you are one type of person, your heart will not feel at ease | Conditional |
| 4 | Siamna nei mi **a om kei leh**, i nasep pen kuitungin muibun tuan het lo thei lai hi | If there is no wise person, our work cannot be completely managed | Conditional (negative) |

**Pattern:** `[Condition] leh [consequence].`

**Analysis:**
- `leh` = and/if (conditional)
- Can be positive (`leh`) or negative (`leh` with `kei`)
- Introduces a conditional subordinate clause

---

### "ahihleh" — If it is

**Function:** Marks conditional clauses with copular "be" — "if it is the case that."

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Ei minam i hamphatna **ahihleh**, hih vai thupi teng khempeuh BSM leh ZCLS in vaipuak hong na laksak a... | If it is our nation's development, in this matter, all the important things, BSM and ZCLS have taken the offering... | Conditional (copular) |

**Pattern:** `[Subject] [predicate] ahihleh, [consequence].`

**Analysis:**
- `ahih` = be (3SG)
- `leh` = if
- Together = "if it is"
- More formal than bare `leh`

## 3. Causal/Purpose Clauses

### "ci-in" (Purposive) — In order to / For

**Function:** Marks purpose clauses — "in order to" do something.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Pasian nasepna **ci-in** na hawm keei mawk leh | **for** God's work, you print it fully | Purpose |
| 2 | I theih limlim dingin Lai Siangtho buppi ei pau ngiat tawh i neih theih **nadingin** sum leh pai tampi banah... | For us to know carefully, the entire Bible, with our mouth alone, to be able to have, with much money and effort... | Purpose (extended) |

**Pattern:** `[Purpose] ci-in/ nadingin [action].`

**Analysis:**
- `ci-in` = quotative-purposive
- `nadingin` = for (purpose)
- Introduces purpose/motivation clauses

---

### "Tua manin" / "Tua hi a" — Therefore / That is why

**Function:** Introduces causal conclusions — "therefore" or "that is why."

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Tua manin** zahtakhuai ka sa semsem hi | **Therefore**, I continuously take permission | Causal conclusion |
| 2 | **Tua hi a**, i Tedim Lai Siangtho pen inntek vaipuak hong na laksak pen a cil pek panin BSM leh ZCLS hi a... | **That is why**, our Tedim Bible, the offering of sincerity that was taken, from which it was said, is BSM and ZCLS... | Causal conclusion (emphatic) |
| 3 | **Ahihmanin** vaipuak a na la i makaite pen zahtakna i piak siam ding pen thupi hi | **Therefore** the offering that was taken and given to us, the permission we must give is important | Causal conclusion |

**Pattern:** `Tua manin/ Tua hi a/ Ahihmanin, [conclusion].`

## 4. Contrastive Clauses

### "Ahizongin" — However / Nevertheless

**Function:** Introduces contrastive clauses — "however" or "nevertheless."

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Ahizongin** a thu paizia thei gegu napi a, "Phalna la nuam lo" "Inntekte thuzak masak ding kisam sa lo" cihte pawl bel... | **However**, a person who understands the meaning of the word, "Doesn't want to take permission" "Doesn't need people to believe first"... | Contrast |
| 2 | **Ahizongin** a thu paizia i tel kul hi | **However**, we must know the meaning of the word | Contrast |

**Pattern:** `Ahizongin, [contrasting point].`

**Analysis:**
- `ahi` = be (3SG)
- `zong` = also/even
- `in` = imperfective
- Together = "however it is also"

---

### "Lem" — Also / Even (Additive Contrast)

**Function:** Adds a parallel or surprising element.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Lem** a kisa lo, leh dik a kisa lo pen... | **Also** it hasn't finished, and rightness hasn't finished yet... | Additive contrast |
| 2 | Haina leh kiphatsakna a kithuah lungsim hi ta a, VAISAH cihin **zong** gen khin zo lo hi | ...shame and offense, even "disaster" **also** cannot be fully spoken of | Additive contrast |

## 5. Cleft/Focus Constructions

### "pen" — Focus/Topic Marker

**Function:** Creates cleft-like emphasis on the preceding element.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Tua **pen** khatvei bek hi lo ding | That **is** only the first time | Focus |
| 2 | A thu paizia **pen**, inntek hihna tawh... | The meaning of the word **is**, with sincerity... | Focus |
| 3 | I Tedim Lai Siangtho **pen**... | Our Tedim Bible **is**... | Focus |
| 4 | A "thu" **pen** a thupi hi zaw hi | Its "word" **is** more important indeed | Focus |
| 5 | Mi neihsa khat peuhpeuh [la, laibu, lawmngaih etc.] tungah khut i khiat nop leh, a neite (inntekte) tawh thudam a limtak gelkhop masak **pen** picinna hi a | Each person themselves, on [book, notebook, etc.], if we desire to touch, with the owner (people), a discussion carefully joined first **is** correct | Focus |

**Pattern:** `[Focused element] pen [predicate].`

**Analysis:**
- `pen` = focus/topic marker
- Creates cleft-like structure: "It is X that..."
- Emphasizes the focused element

---

### "Tua pen" — That is (Cleft)

**Function:** Creates explicit cleft construction.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | **Tua pen** khatvei bek hi lo ding | **That is** only the first time | Cleft |
| 2 | **Tua pen** na pawlpi upa pa khat un... | **That is**, one elder of your community... | Cleft |

**Pattern:** `Tua pen [predicate].`

## 6. Negation in Subordination

### "hi lo" — Negation in Subordinate Clauses

**Function:** Negates elements within subordinate clauses.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | inntek hihna **lo** tawh | without sincerity | Negative prepositional |
| 2 | nang theihpihna **lo** tawh | without your knowledge | Negative prepositional |
| 3 | inntekte theihpihna **lo** tawh | without people's knowledge | Negative prepositional |
| 4 | Thei kha **lo**-a a sem khate | Those who don't know beyond this | Negative participial |
| 5 | Kipilsakna hi zenzen **lo** a | Forcing is absolutely not | Negative copular |

**Pattern:** `[Element] lo [particle].`

**Analysis:**
- `lo` = negation (literary/formal)
- Can negate nouns, verbs, or entire clauses
- Often used with `tawh` (with) to create "without"

---

### Negative Conditional

**Function:** Negates the condition in conditional clauses.

**Examples from article:**

| # | Zolai | English | Function |
|---|-------|---------|----------|
| 1 | Siamna nei mi **a om kei leh**, i nasep pen kuitungin muibun tuan het lo thei lai hi | If there **is no** wise person, our work cannot be completely managed | Negative conditional |

**Pattern:** `[Subject] [verb] kei leh [consequence].`

**Analysis:**
- `kei` = negation
- `leh` = if
- Together = "if not"

## Prose Style Analysis

The copyright article uses **complex subordination** for argumentative structure:

1. **Quotative chains**: `ci-in` → `cih` → `ci-a` report community dialogue
2. **Conditional reasoning**: `leh` → `ahihleh` → `a om kei leh` creates logical flow
3. **Causal conclusions**: `Tua manin` → `Tua hi a` → `Ahihmanin` draw inferences
4. **Contrastive structure**: `Ahizongin` introduces counterpoints
5. **Focus emphasis**: `pen` creates cleft-like prominence
6. **Negation in subordination**: `lo tawh` creates "without" constructions

**Effect:** The complex subordination creates a formal, argumentative prose style appropriate for ethical/legal discussion.

## Key Grammar Notes

### Subordinate Clause Position

Zolai subordinate clauses typically precede the main clause:

```
[Subordinate clause] + [Main clause]
Na hoih i sep leh zong, thu lam tawnin sem ni.
"If you do good also, do it in the manner of the word."
```

### Multiple Subordination

The article chains multiple subordinate clauses:

```
Tua pen na pawlpi upa pa khat un,
"Guai, i sunday school naupangte in hai pana kingah vitamin kisam veh aw"
ci-in ama thu tawh hong lo phei ziau hen la,
Pasian nasepna ci-in na hawm keei mawk leh,
inntek na hihna tawh na lung a kim diam?
```

**Structure:** `[Topic] + [Quote] + [Quotative] + [Purpose] + [Conditional] + [Rhetorical question]`
