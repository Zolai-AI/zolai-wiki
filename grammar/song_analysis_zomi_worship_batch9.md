## 161. Mun Pia (Make Room) — "Make Room"

### Lyrics (excerpt)
```

Kong ki ap hi Topa, kong ki ap hi Topa
Chorus: Mun hong pia ing Topa / Na sepnop bang bang hong sem in
Bridge: Hong lok-khia in ka ngeina hoih lo te / Hong sukham in ka pawlpi lungsim te
```

### Key Lines
| Zolai | Gloss |
|-------|-------|

| Na sepnop bang bang hong sem in | your work whatever do in |
| Hong lok-khia in ka ngeina hoih lo te | remove my bad habits |
| Hong sukham in ka pawlpi lungsim te | renew my congregation hearts |

### New Vocabulary
- **sepnop** — work, task
- **puak** — group, gathering
- **khemna** — desires, wants
- **muanmawhna** — longing, yearning
- **sulzuih** — devoted, committed
- **lok-khia** — remove, take away
- **ngeina** — habits, customs
- **sukham** — renew, restore
- **pawlpi** — congregation

### Themes
Surrender and making room for God — removing old habits, renewing hearts, following God's good way.
