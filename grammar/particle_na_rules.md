# Zolai Grammar: The "na" Particle
> Last updated: 2026-09-10

## Overview
The particle "na" has two main functions in Zolai:
1. **Possessive marker** (your/you)
2. **Noun maker** (turns verbs/adjectives into abstract nouns)

## 1. Possessive "na" (2nd person singular)
Used to indicate possession by "you":

| Form | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| na pa | your father |
| na nu | your mother |
| na tapa | your son |
| na tanu | your daughter |

## 2. Noun-making "na"
Turns verbs and adjectives into abstract nouns:

| Verb/Adj | Noun with "na" | Meaning |
|----------|----------------|---------|
| lungdam | lungdam na | gratitude |
| kum | kum na | age |
| lawm | lawm na | friendship |
| dam | dam na | health |
| thei | thei na | knowledge |
| mu | mu na | sight/vision |
| gen | gen na | speech/language |
| ne | ne na | food |
| sem | sem na | work |

## 3. Common Mistakes

### ❌ WRONG: Using "lungdam na" when thanking
- "Lungdam na!" = "Gratitude!" (noun form) ❌
- This is NOT how you say "thank you"

### ✅ CORRECT: Using "lungdam" when thanking
- "Lungdam!" = "Thank you!" ✅
- This is the verb/interjection form

## 4. Correct Greetings

| Zolai | Meaning | Usage |
|-------|---------|-------|
| Ka dam hi | I am well | Response to "Na dam na?" |
| Lungdam | Thank you | When thanking someone |
| Lungdam mahmah | Thank you very much | Emphatic thanks |
| Na dam na? | How are you? | Greeting question |

## 5. Examples

```
Na dam na? → How are you?
Ka dam hi! → I am well!
Lungdam! → Thank you! (NOT "Lungdam na!")
```
