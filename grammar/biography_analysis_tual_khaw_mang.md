## Full Text (Preserved As-Is)

### Title

Menzi Pu Tual Khaw Mang (B.A, B.L, B.C.S) — Chief Court Advocate

Born: 1935 June 9, Died: 2011 June 8
Birthplace: Saizang, Tedim, Chin State
Parents: Ukpi Chief Thawng Za Khup (A.T.M) + Pi Tel Za Kai

### Pianpih sangngamte (Children's Families)

1. Pi Man Za Go + Pu Song Khan
2. Pi Mang Do Cing + Major Tual Khen Pau
3. Menzi Pu Tual Khaw Mang + Nu Cing Ngo Niiang
4. Lt.Colonel Lian Khan Thang + Nu Hau Khan Lun (Daw Khin Hnin Aye)
5. Pi Lian Khan Vung + Lt.Col.Tual Do Dal
6. Pu Nang Cin Pau + Pi Ciin Do Niang

### Ziletate (Grandchildren)

5 grandchildren listed

### Tanutapate (Great-grandchildren)

10 great-grandchildren listed

### Pian na (Birth Narrative)

Saizang khua ah suak hi (born in Saizang village)

Pian na: Family lineage, 10 siblings listed

Mualbeem khua Zeya Kyaw Htin Lt. Col Lian Cin Zam + Pi Ciin Za Ngul te tanu u pen

### Education

1941 kum in Saizang ah sangkah kipan (started schooling in Saizang)

Tedim, St John's Diocesan High School le Tedim State High School ah kah hi

1954 kum in ZangKong Cushing High School panin tan sawm zo in Rangoon University ah Zato lam sin dingin L.Sc. kah hi

B.A. kahin English Literature, Political Science le Western History tawh 1959 kumin B.A. zo hi

1961 kumin Upadi lam B.L. (Bachelor of Law) degree ngah hi

### Nasepna (Career)

1960-1961: Rangoon University ah English Tutor seem hi

1961-1963: Pegu Division ah nasep sin na a neih khit teh Matupi Township ah Myo-ok le Third Class Magistrate Power ngah hi

1963-1965: Mandalay Division, Yamethin Township ah Second Class Magistrate power tawh S.D.O ngah hi

1965-1968: Maymyo ah District Treasury Officer, Head quaters Magistrate leh Registrar of Deeds seem hi

1968-1971: Amarapura ah Speicial Magistrate Power tawh S.D.O seem hi

1971-1972: Mawlaik ah S.D.O seem hi

1972-1976: Hakha ah ki puak in Constitution thak tawh kisai mipi lungsim laakna leh Hakha Township Election Commission ah Chairman seem hi

1976-1978: Tedim Township people's Council ah zumuk hi

1978-84: People's Council nihna kiteel na (Election Commission) chairman tavuan kipia ban-ah Chin State People's Council ah member in ki teel hi

1984-1988: Zakhang in Sagaing Division zumpi ah Mangpi seem hi

1988-1991: Kalay District ah (Dy. Commissioner) Mangpi seem hi

1991-1993: Kachin Gam Putao District ah Mangpi seem hi

1993-1995: Chin State, Haka ah Menzi (Commissioner) seem hi

1995-1996: Magwe Division ah Menzi (Commissioner) seem hi

1996-1998: Director, Ministry of Home Affairs sem hi

1998 kum June kha in pension la hi

### Mipi aa ding a sep na (Community Development)

1976- Tedim ah a sep sungin UNICEF huhna sialtaang luitui laakna ah vaaihawm hi

Tedim khuakim, Saizang, Kapteel khua te ading zong tui ngah pih hi

1989- Kalay District ah Mangpi a sep laiin Kalemyo pan Monywa mawtaw lamthak bawl ding tungsiah ah a puak tohna tawh kizui in "Yagyi" lampi a kibawl hi

Auto Telephone kideih mah aa, Tedim ciangciang in ngah kei nuteh hong kicih san hi

Pension a laak khit ciangin Zangkong bulphuh in, a gam le a lei khantohna ding a deih sakna tawh mun tuamtuam pan huhna ngah theihna ding vaihawm sak hi

### Biakna Upna (Religious Life)

Zogam khanglui biakna abul mahmah panin khangkhia ahih manin Zo ngeina lui, lasakna, laamna, zu le sa neekna te ah nuamsa mahmah hi

1998 kumin US, Hawaii Haggai Institute te' ah "Advance Christian Leader Seminar" kah hi

Faith Bible Training Centre ah Diploma zong ngah hi

Saizang khua a apu apa te lamsa innpi peen, tu in AG Biakinnpi leh Ciimnuai Childcare Centre, naupang pattahna sang in zangh in a lungkim mahmah hi

### Topa' kiang zuat na (Death)

2011 kum June kha ni 8 ni, nitaak nai 8:30PM hunin Topa' aang sungah a tawntung a tawlnga dingin hong zuat san hi

## Grammar Patterns Observed

### Pattern 1: Ergative Construction [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `NP-ERG + NP-ACC + V + hi`
**Example:** `Zakhang in Sagaing Division zumpi ah Mangpi seem hi`
**Analysis:** `Zakhang` (agent) + `in` (ergative) + `Sagaing Division` (locative) + `Mangpi seem hi` (verb phrase).

### Pattern 2: Locative Postposition [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `NP + ah + V`
**Example:** `Saizang khua ah suak hi`
**Analysis:** `Saizang khua` (place) + `ah` (locative) + `suak hi` (was born). The locative `ah` marks the location of the action.

### Pattern 3: Temporal + Locative Sequence [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `TIME + in + PLACE + ah + V`
**Example:** `1941 kum in Saizang ah sangkah kipan`
**Analysis:** `1941 kum` (time) + `in` (ergative) + `Saizang` (place) + `ah` (locative) + `sangkah kipan` (started schooling). Time phrase precedes location.

### Pattern 4: Completive Aspect [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `V + zo + hi`
**Example:** `B.A. zo hi`
**Analysis:** `B.A.` (object) + `zo` (completive) + `hi` (declarative). `zo` marks the action as completed.

### Pattern 5: Causal Subordination [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `CAUSE + ahih manin + RESULT`
**Example:** `Zogam khanglui biakna abul mahmah panin khangkhia ahih manin Zo ngeina lui... ah nuamsa mahmah hi`
**Analysis:** Complex causal construction with `ahih manin` (because) connecting cause and result.

### Pattern 6: Passive Voice [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `NP + ki + V + hi`
**Example:** `lampi a kibawl hi`
**Analysis:** `lampi` (road) + `a` (3SG.AGR) + `ki` (passive) + `bawl` (build) + `hi` (declarative). The `ki` prefix marks passive voice.

### Pattern 7: Honorific Directionals [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `hong + V + san`
**Example:** `hong zuat san hi`
**Analysis:** `hong` (honorific directional) + `zuat` (pass away) + `san` (honorific suffix). Honorifics used for death.

### Pattern 8: Nominalized Clauses [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `V + na + te`
**Example:** `nasep sin na a neih khit teh`
**Analysis:** `nasep sin` (work) + `na` (nominalizer) + `a neih` (having) + `khit teh` (after). Nominalization creates subordinate clauses.

### Pattern 9: Plural Marking [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `NP + te`
**Example:** `apu apa te` (grandparents), `khua te` (villages)
**Analysis:** `te` marks plural. Used for family members, villages, and other nouns.

### Pattern 10: Compound Word Formation [GRAMMAR]
**Pattern:** `N + N` or `N + V`
**Example:** `lamthak` (road: `lam` path + `thak` new), `lungsim` (sentiment: `lung` heart + `sim` mind)
**Analysis:** Compound words combine two morphemes to create new meanings. Common in career titles and abstract concepts.
