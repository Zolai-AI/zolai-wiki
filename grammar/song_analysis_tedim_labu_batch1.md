## Song 1: PASIAN A MINTHANG HEN! (TDM - 1)

**English:** To God be the Glory!  
**Reference:** Late 29:2  
**Structure:** V1 → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text

**Verse 1:**
Pasian Amin thang hen, na lian pi bawl hi,
Leitung A it man in, A Ta hong pia hi;
Mawhna seel nading in, A hin na paai a,
Mi khempeuh lut nading hinna kong hong ta.

**Verse 2:**
A si in hong lei zo, tatna ki cing khin,
A um te ading in, Pasian in ciam khin;
Mi mawh pen nangawn in Jesuh a up leh,
A mawhna mai ding hi, a up khit phet teh.

**Chorus:**
Phat ve un! Phat ve un! Leitung teng kizel hen!
Phat ve un! Phat ve un! Mi khempeuh lungdam hen!
Jesuh min suang in A Pa' kiang va zuan un;
A bawlsa na lianpi hang in phat ciat un.

**Verse 3:**
Na lianpi hong bawl sak in, ei hong hilh a,
Tapa Jesuh hang in, i lungdam lua ta;
A mai i muh ciang in nuamzaw lai ding hi,
Sang zaw, siang zaw, lian zaw, lungdam zaw ding hi.

### Word-by-Word (Key Lines)

| Line | Zolai | Morpheme Break | English |
|------|-------|----------------|---------|
| V1 | Pasian Amin thang hen | Pasian + A (3SG.AGR) + min (name) + thang (glory) + hen (let) | Let God's name be glorious |
| V1 | Mi khempeuh lut nading hinna kong hong ta | mi (person) + khem+peuh (all) + lut (enter) + na+ding (NMZ+FUT) + hin+na (sin+NMZ) + kong (1SG.OBJ) + hong (DIR) + ta (come) | He came to deliver all people from sin |
| C | Phat ve un! Leitung teng kizel hen! | phat (praise) + ve (EMPH) + un (PL.IMP) + lei+tung (earth+top) + kizel (together) + hen (let) | Praise! Let all earth praise together! |
| C | A bawlsa na lianpi hang in phat ciat un | a (3SG) + bawl+sa (create+STATE) + na (NMZ) + lianpi (forever) + hang (still) + phat (praise) + ciat (ever) + un (PL.IMP) | Let His creation praise forever |
| V3 | Sang zaw, siang zaw, lian zaw, lungdam zaw ding hi | sang (high) + zaw (INTENS) + siang (holy) + zaw + lian (great) + zaw + lungdam (peace) + zaw + ding (FUT) + hi | Higher, holier, greater, more peaceful it will be |

### New Vocabulary
| Word | English | Notes |
|------|---------|-------|
| bawlsa | creation | bawl+sa (create+STATE) |
| kizel | together | ki+zel (RECIP+assemble) |
| ciat | ever/always | temporal intensifier |
| nuamzaw | more joyful | nuam+zaw (joy+INTENS) |
| phatna | praise | phat+na (praise+NMZ) |

### Themes
- God's glory and creation; Christ's sacrifice for sin
- Eternal praise across all earth
- Graduated joy: sang zaw, siang zaw, lian zaw, lungdam zaw
