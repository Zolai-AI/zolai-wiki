## 121. Jesuh' SINGLAMTEH NUAI AH
**English:** Beneath the Cross of Jesus

**Lyrics:**
Verse 1
Jesuh singlamteh nuai ah, Kei mah ka khawlnuam hi;
Ni a sat phatna gam sungah, Suangpi liim bangh ahi;
Gimna le tawlna gam sungah, Tawldamna innpi hi;
Vangik pua te'n van khia dinga, A gim te' khawl na'ng hi.

Verse 2
Jesuh singlamteh liim mah, Khawlmun nuam pen ahi;
Vantung itna le thu manna, Kituah na mun ahi;
Misiangtho te'n a mang sungah, A muh kahlei bangh hi;
Tuasing khuam kei a'ding hi a, Vantung kahlei ahi.

Verse 3
Singlamteh galmuh khop ah, Nilim liap khat om hi;
A lipkhap huai hankhuk hi a, Zai in thuk mahmah hi;
Tua hankhuk le ei kikal ah, Sing lamteh khat om hi;
Singlamteh in lam hong lak a, Vangam lam hong hilh hi.

Verse 4
Jesuh'n singlamteh tung ah, Kei tang gimna thuak hi;
A thuak gimna ka ngaihsun a, Hong itna ka mu hi;
Khitui luang kawm ka lungsim ah, Thu nih ka phawk thei hi;
Ka mawhna le A hong itna, Hih thu te-gel ahi.

Verse 5
Jesuh'n singlamteh liim mah, Kei ma omna ahi;
Amai vangtang lo ngal van-ah, Nitang ki sam lo hi;
Leitung nopsakna le hauhna, Ka lunggulh nawn kei hi;
Ka mawhna te zum na hi a, Singlamteh meetna hi.

**Word-by-word:**
| Line | Zolai | Breakdown |
|------|-------|-----------|
| V1 L1 | Jesuh singlamteh nuai ah | Jesuh = Jesus; singlamteh = cross; nuai = beneath; ah = locative |
| V1 L3 | Ni a sat phatna gam sungah | Ni = day; a sat = its light; phatna = glory; gam = land; sung = in |
| V3 L6 | Singlamteh in lam hong lak a | Singlamteh = cross; in = ergative; lam = path; hong lak = took |

**New vocabulary:** khawlnuam (pleasant place), lipkhap (sound of steps), meetna (foundations), hankhuk (dust), kahlei (level/plain), tawldamna (peace)

**Themes:** Meditative reflection on the cross; sacrifice, love, and redemption through Jesus' suffering.
