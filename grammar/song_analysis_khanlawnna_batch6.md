## Song 101: SI NAWLNO DING

**English:** The Resurrection Day  
**Structure:** Verse → Chorus → V1 → Chorus → V2 → Chorus → V3

### Full Text
**V1:** Lawpgige ing zingsang khat peuh teh, Gabriel pengkul tum ke'n za ning; Sinawnloding, ka khanlawh ciang; It huai Tanpa mu ding ka dawn teh, lungdam awng in lei nusia ning, Sinawnloding ka khanlawh ciang.
**Chorus:** (Sinawnlo ding) Zingsang khat peuh ciang (ka khanlawh ciang) Suangmanpha kizeem,(lungdam si ning), Minthanna gam ah, (Vantung khuapi ah), HIh thu sutsut ning; A khang akhang-a, tatsa te tawh, Amah bia-in phatkhawm ta ding, Sinawnlo ding ka khanlawh ciang.

**V3:** Akitansa minam khempeuh te tawh, vantung hoihna ke'n velvel ning, Sinawnlo ding, ka khanlawh ciang; Tuidam luanna singkungdum gei-ah, khawlna ding mun ke'n kamuding, Sinawnlo ding ka khan lawh ciang.
