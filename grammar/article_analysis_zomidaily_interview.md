## Full Text (Preserved As-Is)

Zomi Khangno Siavuan Lasiam Mangsan tawh Holimna

1. A masa penin na nu le pa min, na unau sanggam teng uh hong gen dih o U Mangsan?

Ka pa bel Rev. Dr. Sian Za Lal (Zogam Lalpu) leh ka nu pen Nu Lam Za Cing hi. Unau 4 pha ung. Ka u Tuatuang (Stephen Vocal Band) leh ka naute numei 2 hi in sang kah lai uh. A u zaw B.E (Civil), a nau Economics University akah laitak uh hi.

2. Koi ah suak a, tu laitak koi ah teng na hia?

Tedim ah suak ing. Tulaitak pen Yangon ah teng ung.

3. Sang koi ah kah na hia?

University of Medicine, Magwe pan 2009 kumin M.B.B.S tawh sang na man ing.

4. Music lam na kipat cilna hong gen dih ve?

Ka neu tung pan hi mai, 1996 kum ka tan 5 lai khawngin Drum leh Guitar khawng na tum khin ing. Sang kah sung 2009 kumin zong Kawlla khat lawm te 4 tawh kipawlin "အလင္းႏွစ္" ci-in khui khat na thoh ngei ung. Tuateh "ZOMI EXODUS" ah tum leh sak, "Van Daitui Ciim" ah zong tum leh sak thuahin na kihel ngei ing.

5. Tu a na lakhum Album thak pen hong pian khiat dan koici bang e U Mangsan?

A masa penpenin ka gen nop ah hih khui sunga late pen ka pa la phuah masak penpente lak pan hoih ka sak teng ka sak kik hi a, tua lo "Sansiip" khui sunga ka sak a thak dawng 2 leh a dang a thak dawng 2 ka beh lap kik hi in a thulu ding bel "THUPHA NGAH MINAM-The Blessed Nation" na ci ing.

## Grammar Patterns

### 1. Interview Question Pattern
- **Pattern**: Topic + `penin` + Question + `dih o` + Name?
- **Example**: `A masa penin na nu le pa min... hong gen dih o U Mangsan?`
- **Note**: `dih o` = polite request form (hortative + vocative)

### 2. First Person with "ka"
- **Pattern**: `ka` + Noun/Possessive
- **Example**: `Ka pa bel...`, `Ka nu pen...`, `Ka neu tung pan`
- **Note**: `ka` = 1st person possessive/agreement marker

### 3. Quotative with "ci-in"
- **Pattern**: "..." + `ci-in` + Verb
- **Example**: `"အလင္းႏွစ္" ci-in khui khat na thoh ngei ung`
- **Note**: `ci-in` = quotative particle (shorter form of `ci`)

### 4. Temporal with "kum" + Year
- **Pattern**: Year + `kum` + Month/Season
- **Example**: `1996 kum ka tan 5 lai khawngin`
- **Note**: `kum` = year marker (very common: 22,880 occurrences)

### 5. Polite First Person with "ung"
- **Pattern**: Statement + `ung`
- **Example**: `Tedim ah suak ing`, `Yangon ah teng ung`
- **Note**: `ing`/`ung` = polite first person marker (interview/formal context)
