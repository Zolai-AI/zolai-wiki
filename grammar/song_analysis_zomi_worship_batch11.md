## 201. Nong Hoih Luat Man Hi E — "Your Goodness Is Enough"

### Lyrics
```
Verse 1: Dahna meipi in, hong tuam ciang / Nawk zawh na ding tha ken nei keng / Nang tawh lo in Topa, kei hi thei keng / Lungnopna lametna, hong bei in / It leh ngaihte, sinthu dang zong / Kei guak hi lo, Zeisu hong heh nem den
Chorus: Ka phawk loh ciang zong hong ompih / To Zesu lo belh ding dang nei keng / Ka hihna khempeuh hong tel siam / Amah bek / Lungdam luankhi nul ing e / Hih zah a kei nong phawk khak zenzen / Ki cinna omlo pi, nong hoih luat man hi e
```

### Key Lines
| Zolai | Gloss |
|-------|-------|
| Dahna meipi in, hong tuam ciang | when trials come |
| Ka phawk loh ciang zong hong ompih | even when I don't understand you provide |
| Ki cinna omlo pi, nong hoih luat man hi e | without conditions, your goodness is enough |

### New Vocabulary
- **dahna** — trial, testing | **meipi** — word, matter | **tuam** — to come upon | **nawk** — to press, pursue | **guak** — to doubt | **ompih** — to provide | **cinna** — condition

### Themes
God's goodness persists through trials and misunderstandings. Unconditional provision.
