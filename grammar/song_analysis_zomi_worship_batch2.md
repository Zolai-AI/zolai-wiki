**Translation:** Oh, the Great Wondrous Love

```

Aw a lamdang itna lian, aw a lamdang itna lian
Calvary pan in hong luang, Hallelujah praise the Lord
I lungdamna tangthu, I gualzawhna sut dih ni
Aw a lamdang itna lian kei sungah

Ka mawhna a tam hang in, Ka khialhna a tam hang in
Tua te sang in hong hehpihna lian zaw lai
Hih thu mah khumpen sang'n, Hih thu mahmah ngaihpen sang'n
A khum pen pen khuaizu sang in khum zaw lai

Ka lametna kip sem sem, Ka lungmuanna kho sem sem
Zeisu hoihna tawh nisim in kei hong zem
Hih thu ka theih nungsang, Upna tawh ka san nungsang

Pasian minthang sem sem hen, Pasian gam zai sem sem hen
Vantung nopna mikim sungah hong tung hen
Tua lamdang lungnopna, I gualzawhna la ngaih pen
Leitung mimal tungah hong ki zeel ta hen
```

**Key line — word by word:**
| Zolai | English | POS |
|-------|---------|-----|
| Mimawh | sinner | N |
| ka hih | I was | PRO+V |
| laitak | helpless | ADJ |

| kei hong lei | you redeemed me | PRO+2SG+V |
| hong dom sang | you carried (me) | 2SG+V |

**New vocabulary:** `mimawh` (sinner), `sipha` (wound), `vangik` (crown), `lametna` (suffering), `lungmuanna` (comfort), `zem` (surpass), `upna` (power), `nungsang` (witness), `gam zai` (sing), `zeel` (spread)
