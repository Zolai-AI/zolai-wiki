# Zolai Sentence Patterns from Zomidaily Corpus

**Source:** 12,966 zomidaily articles (8.7M words)

## Pattern Types

### Simple Sentence (S+V)
**Pattern:** Subject + Verb
**Note:** Most common pattern. Verb comes last (SOV).

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Pasian om hi.`
- `A gen khia hi.`
- `Mi pai ta hi.`

### Transitive (S+O+V)
**Pattern:** Subject + Object + Verb
**Note:** Object precedes verb.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Gam ka mu hi.`
- `Tua thu ka thei hi.`
- `Laisiangtho a ne hi.`

### Ergative (S-ERG+O+V)
**Pattern:** Subject + ergative + Object + Verb
**Note:** Agent marked with 'in' for transitive verbs.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Pasian in leitung a piangsak hi.`
- `Mi in kam a bawl hi.`

### Ditransitive (S+IO+DO+V)
**Pattern:** Subject + Indirect Object + Direct Object + Verb
**Note:** Indirect object before direct object.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Pasian in amaute thupha a pia hi.`
- `A mi te ki pia hi.`

### Negation
**Pattern:** kei + Verb + hi
**Note:** 'kei' for all persons. 'lo' also valid.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Ka pai kei hi.`
- `Na pai kei hi.`
- `A pai kei hi.`

### Negation (lo)
**Pattern:** Verb + lo + hi
**Note:** 'lo' standalone, no 'a' agreement.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Pai lo hi.`
- `Gen lo hi.`
- `Mu lo hi.`

### Future
**Pattern:** Verb + ding + hi
**Note:** Future marker 'ding' before verb.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `A pai ding hi.`
- `Ka gen ding hi.`

### Question (Yes/No)
**Pattern:** Verb + hiam
**Note:** Question marker 'hiam' at end.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Na pai hiam?`
- `A gen hi hiam?`
- `Ka mu hi hiam?`

### Question (Content)
**Pattern:** bang hang + Verb + subject + hiam
**Note:** Content question word comes first.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Bang hang pai na hiam?`
- `Bang hang ci na hiam?`

### Progressive
**Pattern:** Verb + lai + hi
**Note:** Progressive aspect marker.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `A ne lai hi.`
- `A gen lai hi.`

### Completive
**Pattern:** Verb + zo + hi
**Note:** Completed action.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `A pai zo hi.`
- `A ne zo hi.`

### Experiential
**Pattern:** Verb + khin + hi
**Note:** Have experienced.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Ka mu khin hi.`
- `Ka thei khin hi.`

### Passive
**Pattern:** ki + Verb
**Note:** Reflexive 'ki' as passive marker.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Ki suhsak hi.`
- `Ki ki pusuak uh hi.`

### Quotative
**Pattern:** ci / cih + Verb
**Note:** Reported speech.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `A ci hi.`
- `Tua cih a gen hi.`

### Imperative
**Pattern:** Verb + particle
**Note:** Commands softened with particles.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Pai aw.`
- `Gen ni.`
- `Hong lo phei hen la.`

### Complex (Temporal)
**Pattern:** ciangin + clause + main clause
**Note:** Temporal clauses with 'ciangin'.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Khuamial sungah dingcip a, khua hongphawkkikvat ciangin...`

### Complex (Causal)
**Pattern:** ziak / ziakin + clause + main clause
**Note:** Causal clauses.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Tua ziakin a hong khia hi.`

### Complex (Conditional)
**Pattern:** leh / a leh + clause
**Note:** Conditional with 'leh'.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Na pai kei a leh...`

### Relative Clause
**Pattern:** relative + noun
**Note:** Relative clauses modify nouns.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `Mi hong pusuak uh hi a, Bawi Hlawnching hi a...`

### Compound Sentence
**Pattern:** clause + leh + clause
**Note:** Two clauses joined by 'leh'.

**Examples from corpus:**
- `A gen hi leh a pai hi.`
