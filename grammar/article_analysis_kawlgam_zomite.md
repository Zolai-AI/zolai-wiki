## Full Original Text

### Section 1: Property Values

Zan diak mahin Zomite khat in tangtawng pan a neihsa huang khat, Ks. tein tul 51 tawh zuak in Zomite khat mah in lei. 
- Ararat Hotel ci leng zongh, Taung Zalat Hotel, Zomite khat in zuak in, Zomite mah ii lei. (Tawmsan man lo ding). 
- Kawlpi aa bogyoke lampi tungah Zomite' innte, huangte gen loh, lampi dang aa te nangawn tampipi man ta.
- Kawlpi bekbek ah zongh Zomite huang leh inn i muh sak sunsunte Ks. tein tul 20 tungsiah a man ding tampi tak om. (A kep khinkhiante uh i theihpih baan loh tampi om thei lai.)
Mandalay, Yangon ah zongh tua bangmah hi.
- Yangon bang, zan diak mahin, Ks. tein tul 28 tawh inn a lei om, Ks. tein tul 55 tawh huang a lei zongh om. Kum tampi a na nei khin sa inn hoih Ks. tein tul 30 val man ding a nei zongh pawlkhat na na om. Ks. tein tul 10 val man condo nei bel tawm nawn dek lo ding cih theih hi mai. Yangon khuapi sungah htat 8 bang a sang, inn bup nangawn a nei om. 
- Mandalay khua sung lam mun hoih ah zongh Htat 3, 3, 4, 6, 6 cih bang innsang a nei ka theih baan pak bek zongh nga om. 
- Mawtaw tein tul val man a nei bel sim zawh di nawn lo, tein tul 10 val man nangawn a tuang om. 
Hih huangte, innte, mawtawte pen neih sunsun tawh a lei uh hilo, khasim aa loh ding bat laak zongh hilo, cash down vive.
Khua tuamtuam ah Hotel nei, private sang (leitung lam, biakna lam) a nei tampi om, inn hoih a nei bel getna vet omlo. 
- Khat bek a nei hilo, tampi a nei kawikawi zongh kiom. Huang khawng a kimat ciang lei zel, zuak kik zel cih danin Ks. tein tul 30, 40 tawh a kipei zongh om. Shwe, diamond tawh sumbawl tampi om, shwe sai nei nangawn om. Pawlpi siate huang letsong, inn lam sak cih dan zongh kiom.
PhD (leitung lam, biakna lam) a kahkah tawh, a lala tawh tampi om, Master bel gen vet loh tam mahmah ta.
- Online tung lel ah kihauh sak huai het lo, kipil sak huai hiau lo.

### Section 2: Social Media Issues

Tua kawmkal ah "like leh share" ciang peuh tawh huih lak ah a buaibuai khawng kiom lai. 
- A pua ah, a taktak ah, a taksuah tuan het lo ding kitot kinialna leh fake account peuh tawh hi a kisa mahmah zongh om. 
- Sum neksim ciang tawh a hauh sawm kiom tuak lai.
Ahih theih laisiah, khantawn or sawtvei a kimang lo ding thu limlim buaipih masak taak vatlo hi. 
- Khantawn or sawtvei kimang thei ding tektek zongh a piang thei or a piang zo dingte bek tawh hun bei, sum bei sak taak hi.
A pil a haute in minam it lo, kin lo a ci om. 
- Buai taak lote tawh a buai lote mah in, pilna zong man, sum zong man uh hizaw sa ing.
Falam, Khalkha, Mizo lamte aa zongh gen khop ding mah tampi om.
Lungmanna launa tamna ah, mi dangte a ut hangin ah
- kei ka lungsim a ut kei hi, kei tupna a sangzawkna hi.

### Section 3: Values

Ldam, Mi pawlkhatte in 
damdamin a nung teltel ding uh thu tawmkhat
(1) Leitung paizia
Thuhoih, thu manpha, thumaan cihte zui aa na a kisem na hi het loin 
- I sep theih sunsun lak pan i sep zawh tan ciat zui aa a lem theipen dingin a kisem tek dan na hizaw sa ing.
Hih a paizia i tel ma, i sep zawh tuan loh ding hang, thu hoih khempeuh kisep sawm, kisem nuam kha thei hi.
Hih thu pen tel lopi-in
thumaan thutak ci aa miteng i deih danin kam i pau sak nop leh, na i sep sak nop leh,
- pahtaak loh mah hau ding, thupit sak loh lah tam ding, hehna, lungkim lohna mah nei ding cih dan hi sa mai ing.
(2) Mite zia
Na hoih sem leh sem lo sangin
a lawhcing leh lawhcing lo in thugenzaw thukhenzaw sa mai ing.
A beisa lai, thu hoih thu manphate sepna sangin mailam ading lametna tungah mite kingazaw sa ing.
- Cih nopna ah; a beisa aa na lianpi, vai thupi tampi a semsem khat sang' mailam ah or tuletu ah phattuamna a pia thei ding khat pen mite in teelzaw veve lel uh sa ing.
Hih mihingte zia i tel ma, mite muhna kidon lua kha thei hi. Mite hoih sak ding, thupit sak ding, mite phat ding khawng tawh kibuai lua kha thei hi.
I deihpen leh hoih i sakpen a gen hilo aa, ahi bangbang, a piangpiang thute a gen i hizaw hi.

### Section 4: Biblical Allusion

Ldam , Ka tel khialh leh, hong hilh un
(1)
Lai Siangtho sung aa Israel te Egypt gam pan Canaan ah a paina uh
- Ni 40 sawt ding pen kum 40 kaai kicici
Lai Siangtho sungah ka zon leh, tua dan lah mu zo mahmah kei mai ing.
(2)
Canaan gam lut ma deuh Spy te in a va et kholhna uh a ciah a kuan pen ni 40 sawt
- Israel mite in Canaan gam lut ding pen Pasian a muan zawh loh uh ciang, tua ni 1 pen kum 1 khin sak aa kum 40 suak cih dan bel mu iing.
(A phunphunnate uh sangin a muan zawh loh man uh hizaw leh kilawm sa ing.)
(3)
Tua kum 40 sawt thuah ding a kiseh na mun, Kadesh Barnea pen Egypt gam pan kum 2 bang na pai khin dan uh hi. 
- Pasian in kum 40 a sottoh sak ma limin kum 2 bang a na sawt khin dan uh suak hi.
Kadesh Barnea ah kum 40 a om khit uh ciang, Jordan gun kaan aa Canaan gam lut khit nangawn ah kum 7 kiim bang galdo lai uh ci hi.
(4)
Tua ahih manin Israel te Egypt gam pan a paikhiat uh pan Canaan gam ah nuam tak a ten theih hun uh pen a tawmpen kum 40 sawt cih theih hi.
A zenzenin kum 40 bang khintohna a om lo hi phial mah leh zongh kum kum 9 kum 10 kiim mah a kaai ding dan hi.

1. **Economic displacement** — Zomi people are buying and selling property in Burma at very high prices (28-55 lakh kyats), often with cash only.
2. **Social media corruption** — "like and share" culture leads to deception, fake accounts, and gambling.
