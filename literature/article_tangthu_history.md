## Full Text

Tedim Lai Siangtho Tangthu Tom 
(sut zelzel kul ding tawh kibang -- lest it be distorted)

(Thuciam Thak thuluno nuai, laiguallom nunung nihna leh a nunungpen sunga thute tawm na puah ing --  24/8/2026)

.

Tedim Lai Siangtho tangthu cih ciangin Cope Topa (Rev. J. H. Cope, D.D)' hong patkhiat, Tedim Baptist Association leh Zomi Christian Literature Society in a zoptoh, Zolai tawh kigelh Lai Siangtho thu, ahi hi.

### Thuciam Thak

Cope Topa in 1914 kumin Mate (Matthew) zo hi. 1915 kumin Rangoon khuapi (tu hun Yangon), BFBS (British & Foreign Bible Society) pan laibu-in suakkhia hi. 

Nasep zomsuak a, 1929 kumin Thuciam Thak bup zo hi. "Lai Siangtho Thak" ci-in minvawh hi. Rev. Vial Nang in huh hi, ci-in kiciamteh hi. ABM Press pana kibawl hi a, 1932 kumin suakkhia zo pan hi. 

Tua Thuciam Thak pen 1945 kumin BFBS in ABFMS (American Baptist Foreign Mission Society)' phalna tawh India pan khenkik hi. Rev. S.T. Hau Go in a etphat hi a, bu 1,000 kibawl hi. 1948 leh 1951 kumin zong Rangoon ah kibawl, cih BFBS ciaptehna om hi. 

1967 kum ciangin Bible Society of Burma in bu 4,000 bawl leuleu a, Late (Rev. Kam Khaw Thang, D.D, ii lehkhiat) zong kihel ta hi. 

2010 kumin Rev. Dam Suan Mung, D.D, in Cope Topa Tedim hong tun kumzacin ciaptehna-in 1967 kuma kibawl pen mah, a kibang lianin khenkik hi.

### Thuciam Lui

1960 kuma kipan Tedim Baptist Association' vaihawmna tawh Rev. Kam Khaw Thang, D.D, in Thuciam Lui letkhia hi. Late tawh pan a, 1964 kumin Bible Society of Burma in Late a bu-in bawl hi.

1972 kum panin lehkhiatna ah Rev. Hau Lian Kham in bu 2 (Jeremiah, Jonah), Rev. Go Za Kham, D.D, in bu 3 (Kah La, Daniel, Obadiah) huh uh hi.

Tua bu 5 losiah, a dang bu 34te Rev. Kam Khaw Thang' lehkhiat hi a, 1974 kumin zosiang hi. Thuciam Thak a zomsuak pah ding hi a, ahi zongin Lai Siangtho kingaklah lua ta ahih manin Cope Topa' lehkhiat Thuciam Thak tawh gawmkhop dingin kikhensat hi. 

Cope Topa' Thuciam Thak khetkik vai a nunungpen zong hikha ding ahih manin puah vet lo-in ahi bang lianin khetkik dingin zong kikhensat hi.  

### Lai Siangtho Bup

Cope Topa' lehkhiat Thuciam Thak leh Rev. Kam Khaw Thang' lehkhiat Thuciam Lui bubawl dingin India kipuak a, a entel (proofread) dingin Rev. Khup Za Go kicial hi. Tua ciangin Rev, Khup Za Go leh Rev. G. K. Nang, Ph.D, in ettelna (proofreading) limtak bawl uh a, etphatna (editing) a bawl uh zong om hi. 

1977 kumin Thuciam Lui leh Thuciam Thak a kigawm Lai Siangtho, a laibu-in suakkhia hi. Bu 3,500 kibawl hi. Cope Topa' lehkhiat Thuciam Thak, tawm a kipuah pen, leh TBA pana kiletkhia Thuciam Lui a kigawm hi a, Tedim Lai Siangtho neih masakna ahi hi.

Rev. Kam Khaw Thang in Thuciam Lui a kizawh huna kipan Thuciam Thak zomsuak pah a, a zawh ciangin ama' lehkhiatsa Thuciam Lui zong tawm enpha hi. 

1982 kumin lehkhiatna leh etphatna zosiang a, 1983 kumin a laibu suakhia hi. Bu 10,000 kibawl hi. TBA' vaihawmna tawh Lai Siangtho bup neih masakna ahi hi.

ZCLS a pian ciangin TBA in Lai Siangtho nasepna ZCLS ap hi. ZCLS' vaihawmna tawh Rev. Kam Khaw Thang mah in Lai Siangtho puahphatna bawl a, 2004 kumin Lai Siangtho (Puahphatna) a bu-in suakkhia hi.

Tua khit, 2005 kumin etphatna committee kiseh a, a hun zui-in Tedim Lai Siangtho etphatna kibawl toto hi.

### Thuluanzia Thak

2024 kumin Bible Society of Myanmar leh Zomi Christian Literature Society pankhopna tawh Tedim Lai Siangtho Thuciam Thak khat kibubawl hi. 

Tua Thuciam Thak pen Lai Siangtho a omsate etphatna (edition) hilo, puahphatna (revision) zong ahilo, thuluanzia (version) a thak khat tawh a kiletkhia ahi hi. 

Tua Thuciam Thak ah a simnop ding leh a telnop ding kithupisak ahih sam hangin tua sangin Lai Siangtho a kigelh hun lai-a thu-omzia leh kampauzia cihte ahithei zah theihpih nading kibulphuhzaw hi. Tua hangin kammal bul (Greek-kam leh Mangkam) tawh a kituak theithei ahih ding kihanciam hi.

Tua bangin a kihanciam ciangin zat ngeisa kammalte bek kicing thei nawnlo-in kammal a thak tampi zonbeh zatbeh kul hi. Tua kammalte zongsatsa hilo ahih manin khatveivei a simhak hun, a telhak hun zong om thei hi.

Ahi zongin Lai Siangtho sunga thute ii thunung thuma leh a kammal kizangte limtak a thei nuam a sin nuamte' adingin simlah ding leh tellah ding hilo leuleu hi.
