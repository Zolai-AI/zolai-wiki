## 1. Overview

### Course Structure

| Level | Lessons | Focus | Duration |
|-------|---------|-------|----------|
| Beginner | 1-3 | Basic greetings, numbers, family | 3 weeks |
| Elementary | 4-6 | Present tense, negation, questions | 3 weeks |
| Intermediate | 7-9 | Past tense, future, aspect | 3 weeks |
| Advanced | 10 | Complex sentences, discourse | 1 week |

### Learning Objectives

By the end of this course, students will be able to:

1. Greet people and introduce themselves
2. Count and use numbers
3. Talk about family members
4. Form simple sentences in present tense
5. Ask and answer questions
6. Negate sentences
7. Talk about past and future events
8. Use complex sentences
9. Understand Zolai culture and customs

### Lesson 5: Negation

**Level:** Elementary (A2)
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Objectives:**
- Form negative sentences
- Use `kei` and `lo` correctly
- Negate questions

**Vocabulary (20 words):**

| # | Zolai | English | POS | Example |
|---|-------|---------|-----|---------|
| 1 | `Kei` | Not | NEG | `Pai ka kei hi.` |
| 2 | `Lo` | Not (literary) | NEG | `Pai lo hi.` |
| 3 | `Kei in` | Don't (singular) | NEG | `Pai kei in.` |
| 4 | `Kei un` | Don't (plural) | NEG | `Pai kei un.` |
| 5 | `Kei ding` | Won't | NEG | `Pai ka kei ding hi.` |
| 6 | `Kei a leh` | If not | NEG | `Nong pai kei a leh...` |
| 7 | `Thei lo` | Cannot | NEG | `Ka thei lo hi.` |
| 8 | `Nuam lo` | Don't want | NEG | `Ka nuam lo hi.` |
| 9 | `Duh kei` | Don't want | NEG | `Ka duh kei hi.` |
| 10 | `Pai` | Go | VERB | `Pai ka kei hi.` |
| 11 | `Mu` | See | VERB | `Mu ka kei hi.` |
| 12 | `Ne` | Eat | VERB | `Ne ka kei hi.` |
| 13 | `Un` | Drink | VERB | `Un ka kei hi.` |
| 14 | `Gen` | Say | VERB | `Gen ka kei hi.` |
| 15 | `Thei` | Know | VERB | `Thei ka kei hi.` |
| 16 | `Duh` | Want | VERB | `Duh ka kei hi.` |
| 17 | `Om` | Exist/be | VERB | `Om ka kei hi.` |
| 18 | `Sem` | Work | VERB | `Sem ka kei hi.` |
| 19 | `Lai` | Write | VERB | `Lai ka kei hi.` |
| 20 | `Mu` | Hear | VERB | `Mu ka kei hi.` |

**Grammar Point:**
- `kei` is the standard negation particle for ALL persons
- `lo` is also valid (literary/formal)
- `lo` does NOT take `a` agreement: `Pai lo hi.` (NOT `A pai lo hi.`)
- Negative future: `kei + ding`
- Negative conditional: `kei + a + leh`

**Exercises:**

1. **Negate the sentences:**

| Affirmative | Negative |
|-------------|----------|
| `Pai ka hi.` | `Pai ka kei hi.` |
| `Mu na hi.` | `Mu na kei hi.` |
| `Ne a hi.` | `Ne a kei hi.` |
| `Un i hi.` | `Un i kei hi.` |

2. **Fill in the blanks:**

```
Pai ka _____ hi.      (I don't go.)
Mu na _____ hi.        (You don't see.)
Ne a _____ hi.         (He/she doesn't eat.)
Pai ka _____ ding hi.  (I won't go.)
```

3. **Translate to Zolai:**
- I don't go.
- You don't see.
- He/she doesn't eat.
- We won't drink.
- Don't go! (singular)
- Don't go! (plural)

**Homework:**
- Negate 10 affirmative sentences
- Write 5 negative sentences about your daily life
