## 1. Pronunciation Guide

### 1.1 Consonants

| Letter | IPA | How to Pronounce | Example |
|--------|-----|------------------|---------|
| `p` | /p/ | like English "pat" (unaspirated) | `pau` (speak) |
| `t` | /t/ | like English "top" (unaspirated) | `tui` (water) |
| `k` | /k/ | like English "king" (unaspirated) | `khua` (village) |
| `m` | /m/ | like English "mom" | `mi` (person) |
| `n` | /n/ | like English "no" | `ni` (day) |
| `ng` | /ŋ/ | like English "sing" (no hard g) | `nga` (five) |
| `s` | /s/ | like English "sun" | `sing` (tree) |
| `z` | /z/ | like English "zoo" | `zat` (use) |
| `l` | /l/ | like English "let" | `lam` (way) |
| `h` | /h/ | like English "hat" | `hi` (declarative) |
| `b` | /b/ | like English "bat" | `bawl` (create) |
| `d` | /d/ | like English "dog" | `dam` (well) |
| `g` | /g/ | like English "go" | `gam` (land) |
| `c` | /ts/ | like "cats" (ts sound) | `ciangin` (then) |

### 1.2 Consonant Clusters

| Cluster | IPA | How to Pronounce | Example |
|---------|-----|------------------|---------|
| `kh` | /x/ or /kʰ/ | like Scottish "loch" or aspirated "k" | `khua` (village) |
| `th` | /tʰ/ | aspirated "t" (with puff of air) | `thu` (word) |
| `ph` | /pʰ/ | aspirated "p" (with puff of air) | `phung` (clan) |

### 1.3 Vowels

| Letter | IPA | How to Pronounce | Example |
|--------|-----|------------------|---------|
| `a` | /a/ | like "father" | `an` (rice) |
| `e` | /e/ | like "bed" | `eite` (we) |
| `i` | /i/ | like "see" | `inn` (house) |
| `o` | /o/ | like "go" | `om` (exist) |
| `u` | /u/ | like "moon" | `uh` (3PL) |

### 1.4 Diphthongs

| Diphthong | IPA | How to Pronounce | Example |
|-----------|-----|------------------|---------|
| `ai` | /ai/ | like "eye" | `pai` (go) |
| `au` | /au/ | like "cow" | `pau` (speak) |
| `ei` | /ei/ | like "day" | `eite` (we) |
| `oi` | /oi/ | like "boy" | `hoih` (good) |
| `ui` | /ui/ | like "sweet" | `tui` (water) |

### 1.5 Final Consonants

Only 8 consonants can end a syllable: `-p, -t, -k, -m, -n, -ng, -l, -h`

| Final | How to Pronounce | Example |
|-------|------------------|---------|
| `-p` | lips close (no release) | `kip` (dig) |
| `-t` | tongue touches teeth | `kat` (cut) |
| `-k` | tongue touches palate | `lak` (count) |
| `-m` | lips close | `kham` (blocked) |
| `-n` | tongue touches teeth | `pan` (book) |
| `-ng` | tongue touches palate | `song` (type) |
| `-l` | tongue touches teeth | `khal` (foot) |
| `-h` | breath out | `hi` (declarative) |

### 1.6 Tones

Zolai has **three tones** (important for meaning):

| Tone | Mark | Example | Meaning |
|------|------|---------|---------|
| High | acute (á) | `ká` | bamboo |
| Mid | unmarked (a) | `ka` | 1SG pronoun |
| Low | grave (à) | `kà` | harvest |

**Note:** In standard ZVS 2018 orthography, tones are **not marked** in writing. Context determines meaning. When learning pronunciation, tones are important but spelling is consistent.

| `E, ka ne khin hi.` | Yes, I have eaten. | Response |

| `Pai tapeuh ve maw.` | Please go. | Informal |

## 4. Asking for Help, Directions, Shopping

### 4.1 Asking for Help

| Zolai | English |
|-------|---------|
| `Ka zat thei hiam?` | Can you help me? |
| `Panpih in.` | Help [me]. |
| `Bang hang hiam?` | What? / Pardon? |
| `Khol sak in.` | Say it again. |
| `Siam thei kei hi.` | I don't know. |
| `Ka thei kei hi.` | I don't understand. |
| `Kammal khat dih in.` | Say one word (slowly). |

### 4.2 Asking for Directions

| Zolai | English |
|-------|---------|
| `[Place] bang hang pai hiam?` | How do I get to [place]? |

| `Lam hoih a om hiam?` | Is there a good road? |

### 4.3 Shopping

| Zolai | English |

## 6. Formal vs Informal Speech

### 6.1 Pronoun Usage

| Formal | Informal | When to Use |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| `Kei ka` | `Ka` | I (formal introduces self) |
| `Nang na` | `Na` | You (formal addresses respectfully) |
| `Amah a` | `A` | He/She/It (formal introduces topic) |

### 6.2 Sentence Particles

| Formal | Informal | Function |
|--------|----------|----------|
| `hi` | `ve` | Declarative |
| `hiam` | `hia` | Question |
| `ciangin` | `manin` | Therefore |
| `ahih ciangin` | `ahih manin` | Because |
| `tua` | `ci` | That (conjunction) |

### 6.3 Politeness Levels

| Level | Zolai | English | When to Use |
|-------|-------|---------|-------------|
| Direct | `Pai o.` | Go. | Command to child |
| Polite | `Pai un.` | Go (please). | Polite request |
| Very polite | `Pai hen.` | Please go. | Formal request |
| Extremely polite | `Pai tapeuh ve maw.` | Please do go. | Very formal |

### 6.4 When to Switch Registers

| Situation | Register | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| Talking to elder | Formal | `Kei ka na ne thu ka gen hi.` |
| Talking to friend | Informal | `Na ne thu ka gen ve.` |
| Writing a letter | Formal | `Tua in [name] te ne thu ka gen hi.` |
| Texting a friend | Informal | `Na lungdam na?` |
| Church sermon | Formal | `Pasian in a ci hi.` |
| Market shopping | Informal | `[Item] ka duh hi.` |

## 7. Conversation Examples

### Conversation 1: Meeting Someone New

```
A: Na dam na?
   [How are you?]

B: Dam mah. Na dam na?
   [Fine. How are you?]

A: Ka dam hi. Lungdam.
   [I am well. Thank you.]

B: Na min bang hang hiam?
   [What is your name?]

A: Ka min pen [Name] ahi hi. Na min bang hang hiam?
   [My name is [Name]. What is your name?]

B: Ka min pen [Name] ahi hi.
   [My name is [Name].]

A: Lungdam. Na khua bang hang hiam?
   [Thank you. Where is your village?]

B: Ka khua Tedim ah a om hi.
   [My village is in Tedim.]

A: Lungdam mahmah ka hi.
   [I am very happy.]
```

### Conversation 2: Asking for Directions

```
A: Ka zat thei hiam?
   [Can you help me?]

B: E, bang hang hiam?
   [Yes, what is it?]

A: Tedim College bang hang pai hiam?
   [How do I get to Tedim College?]

B: Tua pen tengah a om hi. Lam khat lam ah pai un.
   [It is nearby. Go along one road.]

A: Tengah hiam?
   [Is it near?]

B: E, tengah a om hi. Ni khat ah ka pai thei hi.
   [Yes, it is near. You can go in one day.]

A: Lungdam.
   [Thank you.]
```

### Conversation 3: At the Market

```
A: [Item] ka duh hi.
   [I want [item].]

B: E, [item] a om hi. Man pen [price] ahi hi.
   [Yes, we have [item]. The price is [price].]

A: Kham ka hi.
   [It is expensive.]

B: Phak na hiam?
   [Do you want to buy?]

A: Phak kei hi. Tenna a om hi.
   [I don't want to buy. It is far.]

B: Hoih. Na lungdam.
   [Okay. Thank you.]

A: Lungdam.
   [Thank you.]
```

### Conversation 4: Family Talk

```
A: Na pa leh na nu hiam?
   [Where are your parents?]

B: Amah te Tedim ah a om hi.
   [They are in Tedim.]

A: Na pianpih te hiam?
   [Where are your children?]

B: Amah te [place] ah a om hi.
   [They are in [place].]

A: Na innkuan hoih hiam?
   [Is your family well?]

B: E, innkuan khempeuh dam hi.
   [Yes, all the family is well.]

A: Lungdam.
   [Thank you.]
```

### Conversation 5: At Church

```
A: Na dam na?
   [How are you?]

B: Dam hi. Na dam na?
   [I am well. How are you?]

A: Ka dam hi. Biakna sungah hong ta ve maw.
   [I am well. Please come to worship.]

B: Lungdam. Tua pen ni hoih ahi hi.
   [Thank you. That is a good day.]

A: E, Pasian in amaute a ngaih hi.
   [Yes, God loves them.]

B: Lungdam mahmah ka hi.
   [I am very happy.]

A: Biakna kipan a ki hi. Pai un.
   [Worship has begun. Let's go.]

B: E, pai ta ning.
   [Yes, I will go now.]
```

## 8. Common Pronunciation Mistakes

### 8.1 Consonant Clusters

| Mistake | Correct | Explanation |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| `khua` pronounced as "kua" | `khua` (khua) | `kh` is a single sound (like Scottish "loch") |
| `thu` pronounced as "tu" | `thu` (thoo) | `th` is aspirated (with puff of air) |
| `phung` pronounced as "pung" | `phung` (phung) | `ph` is aspirated |

### 8.2 Final Consonants

| Mistake | Correct | Explanation |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| `-k` released as "k" | `-k` (unreleased) | Final `-k` is NOT released |
| `-p` released as "p" | `-p` (unreleased) | Final `-p` is NOT released |
| `-t` released as "t" | `-t` (unreleased) | Final `-t` is NOT released |

### 8.3 Vowel Length

| Mistake | Correct | Explanation |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| `a` (short) vs `aa` (long) | `a` (short) | Zolai has short vowels only |
| `e` pronounced as "ee" | `e` (like "bed") | `e` is mid front, not high |

### 8.4 Word Boundary

| Mistake | Correct | Explanation |
|---------|---------|-------------|
| `khat` split as "ka + t" | `khat` (one word) | `khat` is one word |
| `ciangin` split as "ci + angin" | `ciangin` (one word) | `ciangin` is one word |

### 8.5 Common Confusions

| Confusion | Correct | Explanation |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| `gam` vs `ram` | `gam` (Zolai) | `ram` is Hakha/Falam (forbidden in ZVS 2018) |
| `pasian` vs `pathian` | `pasian` (Zolai) | `pathian` is Hakha/Falam (forbidden in ZVS 2018) |
| `tapa` vs `fapa` | `tapa` (Zolai) | `fapa` is Hakha/Falam (forbidden in ZVS 2018) |
| `topa` vs `bawipa` | `topa` (Zolai) | `bawipa` is Hakha/Falam (forbidden in ZVS 2018) |
