## 1. Zolai Sentence Construction Rules

### 1.1 SOV Word Order (Subject–Object–Verb)

The verb **always** comes last. This is the most important rule in Zolai writing.

| Pattern | Correct | Incorrect |
|---------|---------|-----------|
| S + V | `Pasian om hi.` | `Om Pasian hi.` ❌ |
| S + O + V | `Gam ka mu hi.` | `Ka gam mu hi.` ❌ |
| S + IO + DO + V | `Pasian in amaute thupha a pia hi.` | `Pasian thupha amaute a pia hi.` ❌ |

### 1.2 Agreement Markers

Agreement markers (`a`, `ka`, `na`) go **before the verb**, not after the subject:

| Person | Agreement | Example |
|--------|-----------|---------|
| 1SG (I) | `ka` | `Ka pai hi.` (I go.) |
| 2SG (you) | `na` | `Na pai hi.` (You go.) |
| 3SG (he/she/it) | `a` | `A pai hi.` (He goes.) |
| 1PL (we) | `ka` | `Ka pai-te hi.` (We go.) |
| 2PL (you all) | `na` | `Na pai-te hi.` (You all go.) |
| 3PL (they) | `a` | `A pai-te hi.` (They go.) |

### 1.3 Ergative Marker `in`

The ergative `in` marks the **agent** of a transitive verb. It follows the subject:

```
Pasian in vantung a piangsak hi.
[God] [ERG] [heaven] [3SG.AGR] [create] [DECL]
"God created heaven."
```

**When to use `in`:**
- Proper noun subjects: `Pasian in...`, `Jesu in...`
- Full noun phrases: `Mite te in...`, `Numte in...`
- Emphasized or contrasted subjects
- **Optional** with agreement markers (`a`, `ka`, `na`)

**When NOT to use `in`:**
- Pronoun subjects: `Ka pai hi.` (NOT `Kei in pai hi.` ❌)
- Intransitive verbs: `Pasian om hi.` (God exists.) — no `in`

### 1.4 Negation

**Standard negation: `kei`** (for ALL persons):

```
Ka pai kei hi.      I don't go.
Na pai kei hi.      You don't go.
A pai kei hi.       He doesn't go.
```

**Literary/formal negation: `lo`** (standalone, NO `a` agreement):

```
Pai lo hi.          Goes not.
Pai lo ding.        Will not go.
```

**⚠️ CRITICAL:**
- ✅ `Pai lo hi.` — Correct
- ❌ `A pai lo hi.` — WRONG (`lo` does NOT take `a`)

### 1.5 Questions

**Yes/No questions use `hiam`:**

```
Na pai hiam?        Do you go?
Na dam hiam?        Are you well?
```

**Content questions use `bang hang` + verb + subject + `hiam`:**

```
Bang hang pai na hiam?    Why do you go?
Bang hang ne na hiam?     What do you eat?
```

**⚠️ CRITICAL: Content question word order:**
- ✅ `Bang hang pai na hiam?` — Correct
- ❌ `Bang hang na pai hiam?` — WRONG

### 1.6 Tense and Aspect

| Tense/Aspect | Marker | Example |
|--------------|--------|---------|
| Present | `hi` | `A pai hi.` (He goes.) |
| Past | `ta` | `A pai ta hi.` (He went.) |
| Future | `ding` | `A pai ding hi.` (He will go.) |
| Completive | `zo` | `A pai zo hi.` (He finished going.) |
| Experiential | `khin` | `Ka mu khin hi.` (I have seen [before].) |
| Progressive | `lai` | `A ne lai hi.` (He is eating.) |

### 1.7 Sentence-Final Particles

| Particle | Function | Example |
|----------|----------|---------|
| `hi` | Declarative (neutral) | `Ka pai hi.` |
| `ve` | Informal/soft | `Ka pai ve.` |
| `hen` | Polite request | `Hong pai hen.` |
| `un` | Imperative | `Pai un.` |
| `in` | Topic/focus | `Tua in...` |
| `vo` | Emphasis | `A hoih vo!` |

## 2. Paragraph Structure in Zolai

### 2.1 Topic Sentence

Every paragraph begins with a **topic sentence** that introduces the main idea:

```
Zolai thu kammal pen Zomite minam thu ahi hi.
Amah in Tedim Chin kai pen hi.
```
"The Zolai language is the language of the Zomi people. It belongs to the Tedim Chin branch."

### 2.2 Supporting Sentences

Follow the topic sentence with **supporting details**:

```
Zolai thu kammal pen Zomite minam thu ahi hi.
Amah in Tedim Chin kai pen hi.
Mite te leh numei te Zolai thu a pau hi.
Leitung mihing kici khempeuh Zolai thu a thei hi.
```
"The Zolai language is the language of the Zomi people. It belongs to the Tedim Chin branch. Men and women speak Zolai. All people on earth know Zolai."

### 2.3 Concluding Sentence

End with a **concluding sentence** that wraps up the idea:

```
Zolai thu kammal pen Zomite minam thu ahi hi.
Amah in Tedim Chin kai pen hi.
Mite te leh numei te Zolai thu a pau hi.
Tua manin Zolai thu kammal pen hoih mahmah hi.
```
"...Therefore the Zolai language is very good."

### 2.4 Logical Connectors

| Connector | Function | Example |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| `ciangin` | then/therefore | `Ciangin ka pai ta hi.` (Then I went.) |
| `ahih ciangin` | that is why | `A hoih ahih ciangin, ka pai ta hi.` (That is why I went.) |
| `ahih manin` | because (informal) | `A hoih ahih manin, ka pai ta hi.` |
| `tepen` | today | `Tepen ka pai hi.` (Today I go.) |
| `napi` | yesterday | `Napi ka pai ta hi.` (Yesterday I went.) |
| `zingciang` | tomorrow | `Zingciang ka pai ding hi.` (Tomorrow I will go.) |
| `lem` | also/too | `Amah lem ka pai hi.` (He also goes.) |
| `leh` | and (nouns) | `Ka nu leh ka pa` (my mother and father) |
| `a` | and (clauses) | `Ka pai a, na ne hi.` (I go and you eat.) |

### 2.5 Paragraph Length

- **News articles:** 3–5 sentences per paragraph
- **Essays:** 5–8 sentences per paragraph
- **Personal narratives:** 2–4 sentences per paragraph
- **Devotional texts:** 1–3 sentences per paragraph

## 3. Formal vs Informal Writing

### 3.1 Register Comparison

| Feature | Formal | Informal |
|---------|--------|----------|
| Declarative | `hi` | `ve` |
| Question | `hiam` | `hia` |
| "Therefore" | `ciangin` | `manin` |
| "Because" | `ahih ciangin` | `ahih manin` |
| "That" (conjunction) | `tua` | `ci` |
| Pronoun dropping | Full forms | Agreement markers only |
| Sentence length | Longer, complex | Shorter, direct |

### 3.2 Formal Writing Rules

1. **Always use `hi`** (never `ve`)
2. **Use full pronoun forms**: `Kei ka`, `Nang na`, `Amah a`
3. **Use formal connectors**: `ciangin`, `ahih ciangin`, `tua manin`
4. **Complex sentences** with subordinate clauses
5. **Ergative `in`** with organizational agents

### 3.3 Informal Writing Rules

1. **Use `ve`** for casual tone
2. **Drop pronouns** when agreement is clear: `Ka pai ve.` (not `Kei ka pai ve.`)
3. **Use informal connectors**: `manin`, `ci`
4. **Short sentences**, direct statements
5. **Emotional particles**: `mahmah`, `lua`, `dih`

### 3.4 Register Examples

**Formal:**
```
Zomite te zolai siamna panpihna sungah hong kipan ahih ciangin,
copyright ngaih hiam? zomite te thu a siam hi.
```

**Informal:**
```
Zomite zolai siamna ah hong kipan ciangin,
copyright hiam? zomite zatua hi.
```

## 4. Letter Writing Format

### 4.1 Personal Letter (Informal)

```
Na lungdam na?

Kei ka na ne thu ka gen veve hi.
Tepen zingah Tedim ah ka pai ta hi.
Lungdam mahmah ka hi.

Na dam na? Na lungdam maw?
Kei ka na lungdam hi. Lungdam.

Ciah ta ning.
Na ka lungdam.

[Date]
[Name]
```

**Translation:**
```
Hello?

I am writing to you.
Today morning I went to Tedim.
I am very happy.

How are you? Are you well?
I am well. Thank you.

I will go now.
Your friend.

[Date]
[Name]
```

### 4.2 Formal Letter

```
Tepen [date] ni.

Tua in [recipient name] te ne thu ka gen hi.

Tua pen [subject] ahi hi.
Ahih ciangin [main content].

Tua manin [request/conclusion].

Lungdam mahmah ka hi.
Na ka [formal address].

[Sender name]
[Title/Position]
[Date]
```

### 4.3 Letter Components

| Component | Formal | Informal |
|-----------|--------|----------|
| Opening | `Tua in [name] te ne thu ka gen hi.` | `Na lungdam na?` |
| Subject | `Tua pen [subject] ahi hi.` | Direct statement |
| Body | Complex sentences, `ciangin`, `ahih ciangin` | Short sentences, `manin` |
| Closing | `Lungdam mahmah ka hi.` | `Lungdam.` |
| Signature | `[Name], [Title]` | `[Name]` |

## 5. Essay Writing Format

### 5.1 Introduction Paragraph

```
Zolai thu kammal pen Zomite minam thu ahi hi.
Amah in Tedim Chin kai pen hi.
Tua manin zolai thu kammal pen hoih mahmah hi.
```

### 5.2 Body Paragraphs

**Paragraph 1 (Historical Background):**
```
Zolai thu kammal 1902 ah kipan hi.
Rev. Carson in Zolai thu a kizak hi.
Tua ciangin Zolai thu kammal hawmkhia ta hi.
```

**Paragraph 2 (Current Use):**
```
Tua manin tepen zomite te zolai thu a pau hi.
Leitung mihing kici khempeuh zolai thu a thei hi.
Zolai thu kammal panpihna sungah hong kipan hi.
```

**Paragraph 3 (Challenges):**
```
Zolai thu kammal khangui a om hi.
English thu kammal in zolai thu kammal a zat hi.
Ahih manin zomite te zolai thu siamna panpih a ki hi.
```

### 5.3 Conclusion Paragraph

```
Ahih ciangin, zolai thu kammal pen hoih mahmah hi.
Zomite te zolai thu kammal a ngaih hi.
Tua manin zolai thu kammal panpihna sungah hong kipan a ki hi.
```

### 5.4 Essay Structure Template

| Section | Sentences | Connectors |
|---------|-----------|------------|
| Introduction | 3–4 | `Ahih ciangin`, `Tua manin` |
| Body ¶1 | 4–5 | `Ciangin`, `Tua ciangin` |
| Body ¶2 | 4–5 | `Tua manin`, `Ahih manin` |
| Body ¶3 | 4–5 | `Ahih ciangin` |
| Conclusion | 3–4 | `Ahih ciangin`, `Tua manin` |

## 6. Common Writing Errors and Corrections

### 6.1 Word Order Errors

| Error | Correct | Issue |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `Ka gam mu hi.` | `Gam ka mu hi.` | Object before subject |
| `Om Pasian hi.` | `Pasian om hi.` | Verb not at end |
| `Pai ka hi.` | `Ka pai hi.` | Verb before subject |

### 6.2 Agreement Marker Errors

| Error | Correct | Issue |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `Ka pai hi.` (about him) | `A pai hi.` | Wrong person |
| `A pai kei hi.` | `Ka pai kei hi.` (about self) | Wrong person |
| `Na pai hi.` (about her) | `A pai hi.` | Wrong person |

### 6.3 Negation Errors

| Error | Correct | Issue |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `A pai lo hi.` | `Pai lo hi.` | `lo` takes NO `a` |
| `Ka lo pai hi.` | `Ka pai kei hi.` | `lo` is standalone |
| `A pai kei ding hi.` | `A pai kei ding.` | `hi` optional with `ding` |

### 6.4 Question Word Order Errors

| Error | Correct | Issue |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `Bang hang na pai hiam?` | `Bang hang pai na hiam?` | Verb before subject in content Q |
| `Na pai hiam.` | `Na pai hiam?` | Missing question mark |
| `Bang hang na ne hiam?` | `Bang hang ne na hiam?` | Verb before subject in content Q |

### 6.5 ZVS 2018 Forbidden Forms

| Forbidden | Correct | Meaning |
|-----------|---------|---------|
| `pathian` | `pasian` | God |
| `ram` | `gam` | earth, land |
| `fapa` | `tapa` | son/life |
| `bawipa` | `topa` | Lord, master |
| `siangpahrang` | `kumpipa` | Savior |
| `cu/cun` | `tua` | that (conjunction) |
| `suah` | `suahtakna` | holiness (context-dependent) |
| `nunnak` | `nuntakna` | life (context-dependent) |

### 6.6 Ergative Errors

| Error | Correct | Issue |
|-------|---------|-------|
| `Kei in pai hi.` | `Ka pai hi.` | No `in` with pronouns |
| `Pasian om hi.` (transitive) | `Pasian in mu hi.` | Missing `in` for transitive |
| `Mite te a ne hi.` | `Mite te in ne hi.` | Missing `in` for noun agent |

## 7. ZVS 2018 Compliance in Writing

### 7.1 Mandatory Rules

1. **Use `pasian`** (NOT `pathian`) for God
2. **Use `gam`** (NOT `ram`) for earth/land
3. **Use `tapa`** (NOT `fapa`) for son/life
4. **Use `topa`** (NOT `bawipa`) for Lord
5. **Use `kumpipa`** (NOT `siangpahrang`) for Savior
6. **Use `tua`** (NOT `cu/cun`) for "that" conjunction
7. **Use `suahtakna`** (NOT `suah`) for holiness (context-dependent)
8. **Use `nuntakna`** (NOT `nunnak`) for life (context-dependent)

### 7.2 Self-Check Before Publishing

| Check | Pass | Fail |
|-------|------|------|
| All `pasian` spelled correctly | ✅ | `pathian` ❌ |
| All `gam` spelled correctly | ✅ | `ram` ❌ |
| All `tapa` spelled correctly | ✅ | `fapa` ❌ |
| All `topa` spelled correctly | ✅ | `bawipa` ❌ |
| All `kumpipa` spelled correctly | ✅ | `siangpahrang` ❌ |
| All `tua` spelled correctly | ✅ | `cu`/`cun` ❌ |
| SOV word order throughout | ✅ | SVO ❌ |
| `lo` without `a` agreement | ✅ | `A pai lo hi.` ❌ |
| Content Q: verb before subject | ✅ | `Bang hang na pai hiam?` ❌ |

## 8. Sample Texts

### Text 1: Formal Letter (Request)

```
Tua in Tedim College te ne thu ka gen hi.

Tua pen zolai thu kammal panpihna ahi hi.
Ahih ciangin, zomite te zolai thu kammal siamna panpih sungah
hong kipan a ki hi.

Tua manin, zolai thu kammal panpihna kammal khat ka zat thei hiam?
Zomite te innkuan leh minam it sungah hong om a hi.

Lungdam mahmah ka hi.
Na ka [Name].
```

### Text 2: Essay (Zolai Language)

```
Zolai thu kammal pen Zomite minam thu ahi hi.
Amah in Tedim Chin kai pen hi.
Mite te leh numei te Zolai thu a pau hi.

Zolai thu kammal 1902 ah kipan hi.
Rev. Carson in Zolai thu a kizak hi.
Tua ciangin Zolai thu kammal hawmkhia ta hi.

Tua manin tepen zomite te zolai thu a pau hi.
Leitung mihing kici khempeuh zolai thu a thei hi.
Zolai thu kammal panpihna sungah hong kipan hi.

Zolai thu kammal khangui a om hi.
English thu kammal in zolai thu kammal a zat hi.
Ahih manin zomite te zolai thu siamna panpih a ki hi.

Ahih ciangin, zolai thu kammal pen hoih mahmah hi.
Zomite te zolai thu kammal a ngaih hi.
Tua manin zolai thu kammal panpihna sungah hong kipan a ki hi.
```

### Text 3: News Report

```
Tepen Tedim ah zolai thu kammal panpihna kammal khat kipan ta hi.
Zomite te zolai siamna panpihna sungah hong kipan ahih ciangin,
copyright ngaih hiam? zomite te thu a siam hi.

Tua manin zomite te innkuan leh minam it sungah hong om ahih ciangin,
copyright zomite te thu a siam lo hi.

Ahih ciangin, zomite te zolai thu kammal siamna panpih sungah
hong kipan a ki hi. Lungdam mahmah ka hi.
```

### Text 4: Devotional

```
Pasian in vantung leh leitung a piangsak hi.
Amah in mite leh numei a bawl hi.
Amah in amaute thupha a pia hi.

"A thu hoih a hi hi," Pasian in a ci hi.
Mite te pasian kibang a om thei kei hi.
Pasian in amaute a ngaih hi a, amaute in Pasian a ngaih hi.

Ahih ciangin, mite te Pasian a ngaih thei hi.
Pasian in amaute a ngaih hi a, amaute in Pasian a ngaih hi.
Lungdam mahmah ka hi.
```

### Text 5: Personal Narrative

```
Kei ka Tedim ah ka pai ta hi.
Tepen zingah ka nu leh ka pa kiton ta hi.
Lungdam mahmah ka hi.

Amah te ka ne ta hi.
Ka zolai thu a pau ta hi.
Ka lungdam a hi hi.

Napi ah ka Tedim ah ka pai ta hi.
Ciangin ka nu leh ka pa kiton ta hi.
Lungdam mahmah ka hi.

Zingciang ka pai ding hi.
Na lungdam maw?
Kei ka na lungdam hi. Lungdam.
```
