## 1. Overview

### Test Structure

| Test | Items | Points | Time |
|------|-------|--------|------|
| Vocabulary | 50 words | 100 points | 15 minutes |
| Grammar | 30 sentences | 120 points | 20 minutes |
| Reading Comprehension | 5 passages (25 questions) | 100 points | 30 minutes |
| Translation | 20 sentences | 80 points | 25 minutes |
| Writing | 5 prompts | 100 points | 30 minutes |
| Speaking | 10 topics | 100 points | 20 minutes |
| **Total** | — | **600 points** | **140 minutes** |

### Proficiency Levels

| Level | CEFR | Score Range | Description |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------|
| Beginner | A1 | 0-150 | Basic greetings, simple phrases |
| Elementary | A2 | 151-250 | Simple sentences, basic grammar |
| Intermediate | B1 | 251-350 | Complex sentences, most grammar |
| Upper Intermediate | B2 | 351-450 | Fluent conversation, nuanced grammar |
| Advanced | C1 | 451-550 | Near-native proficiency |
| Mastery | C2 | 551-600 | Native-level competence |

## 6. Writing Test

### Instructions

Write a short paragraph (3-5 sentences) for each prompt. Use correct ZVS 2018 orthography and proper grammar.

### Prompts

**1. Describe your family.**
Write about your family members (parents, siblings, etc.) in Zolai.

**2. Describe your daily routine.**
Write about what you do every day (morning, afternoon, evening).

**3. Write a story.**
Write a short story about something that happened to you or someone you know.
