# Compound Words Reference

## Overview

Zolai compound words are formed by combining two or more free morphemes. This reference lists common compounds with their component breakdowns and meanings. All entries use correct ZVS 2018 orthography.

## Compound Word List

### 1. laisiangtho (Bible)

| Component | Morpheme | Meaning |
|-----------|----------|---------|
| `lai` | — | book/text/document |
| `siang` | — | clean/holy/pure |
| `tho` | suffix | — |

**Compound:** `lai` (book) + `siang` (holy) + `tho` (suffix) = "holy book" = Bible

**Note:** `lai` means "book/text/document" — NOT "write." The verb "write" is `sih`.

## Notes

- **`lai` means "book/text/document"** — NOT "write." The verb "write" is `sih`.
- **`siang` means "clean/holy/pure"** — adjective form used in compounds.
- **`tho` is a suffix** — used to form adjectives from nouns/adjectives.
- **`lamthak` is a common compound** — `lam` (path) + `thak` (new) = road.
- **`bulphuh`** — important cultural term for "hometown" or "ancestral place."
- **`-na` suffix** — creates abstract nouns from verbs (e.g., `laam` → `laamna`).
- All compound words follow ZVS 2018 orthography.
- New compounds (21-30) extracted from biography of Pu Tual Khaw Mang.
