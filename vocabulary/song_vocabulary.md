# Song & Hymn Vocabulary Reference

> 80+ vocabulary entries extracted from 987 Zolai worship songs.
> All entries use correct ZVS 2018 orthography.
> Last updated: 2026-09-9

---

## Overview

This reference catalogues vocabulary found in Zolai worship song lyrics, organized by semantic category. Songs use a **elevated liturgical register** that differs from everyday speech — many terms appear primarily in worship contexts.

**Source corpus:**
- Khanlawnna Late: 202 songs
- Tedim Labu: 508 songs
- Zomi Worship Collective: 277 songs

## Notes

- **All entries use ZVS 2018 orthography** — `pasian` (not `pathian`), `gam` (not `ram`), `tapa` (not `fapa`)
- **Song vocabulary is elevated** — many terms are liturgical, not everyday speech
- **Compound words are productive** — new compounds can be formed by combining morphemes
