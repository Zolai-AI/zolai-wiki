## Key Findings

1. **Modern vocabulary:** The corpus contains many modern terms (Malaysia, USA, COVID, etc.) not found in traditional Bible corpus
2. **Discourse markers:** High frequency of emphasis particles (mahmah, uh, ahihi, cihi) indicates conversational style
3. **Temporal system:** Rich temporal vocabulary (tepen, napi, abeisa, akipan) for recent past
4. **Loan words:** Significant borrowing from English/Malay for modern concepts
5. **Named entities:** Many proper nouns (places, organizations, people) reflecting current events
