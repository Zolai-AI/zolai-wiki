---

## Overview

Zolai idioms (`Gentehna`) are deeply rooted in nature, domestic life, agriculture, and spiritual journeys. They often use the `lung` (heart/mind) compound system to express emotions and the natural world as metaphor for human experience.

## Usage Notes

- **Register is critical:** Using church language (`laisiangtho pau`) in casual conversation sounds stiff. Using casual speech (`pau dan`) in prayer is disrespectful.
- **`lung` compounds** are the primary vehicle for emotional expression — learn these first.
- **Fixed phrases** like `Tua ciangin`, `Tua ahih manin`, `Ahi zongin` are essential for formal writing and speaking.
- **Conversational expressions** like `lungdam ka hi`, `dam na hi hiam?` are essential for daily interaction.
- **Religious idioms** (`hotkhiatna`, `hehpihna`, `singlamteh`) are essential for church contexts.
