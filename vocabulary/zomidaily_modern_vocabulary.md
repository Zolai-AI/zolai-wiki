## 7. Modern Sentence Patterns

### Complex Sentences (Modern Zolai)

```
BSM in Tedim Lai Siangtho bu 5000 pen Bible Society Myanmar kiang ah ap na na nei thei ing.
[BSM] [ERG] [Tedim Bible] [book 5000] [TOP] [BSM] [toward] [hand over] [2SG] [can] [IMPF]
"BSM can hand over 5000 copies of the Tedim Bible to Bible Society Myanmar."
```

### Loan Word Integration

```
Facebook tungah post khat ka post hi.
[Facebook] [on] [post] [one] [1SG] [post] [DECL]
"I posted a post on Facebook."
```

### Modern Discourse Chain

```
Tua banah, ahi zongin, ahizongin, peuhmah, inzong...
[moreover] [also] [however] [too] [even]...
```
