## 1. Greetings & Introductions

| Zolai | English | Situation | Formality |
|-------|---------|-----------|-----------|
| `Na dam na?` | How are you? | Meeting someone | Formal |
| `Dam maw?` | How are you? | Meeting friend | Informal |
| `Ka dam hi.` | I am well. | Response | Formal |
| `Dam mah.` | Fine. | Response | Informal |
| `Zingsang pha.` | Good morning. | Morning greeting | Modern |
| `Nitak pha.` | Good evening. | Evening greeting | Modern |
| `Ni hoih.` | Good day. | General greeting | Formal |
| `An na ne na?` | Have you eaten? | Common greeting | All |
| `E, ka ne khin hi.` | Yes, I have eaten. | Response | All |
| `An ne nailo hing.` | I haven't eaten yet. | Response | All |
