## 6. Tips for Disambiguation

1. **Check the grammatical context:** Is the word used as a noun, verb, adjective, or particle?
2. **Check surrounding words:** What words appear before and after?
3. **Check the register:** Is this formal writing, casual speech, or biblical text?
4. **Check for reduplication:** `saisai` (quietly) vs `sai` (one sense)
5. **Use the Bible corpus:** Search for the word in context to see actual usage
